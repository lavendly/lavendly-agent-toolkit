# Prompt anchors: storyteller

Vocabulary for the one shot prompt and the one script. Concrete language in,
adjectives out.

## Shot prompt template

```text
<subject in one clause: who or what, age or scale, wardrobe or material, colour>
<setting in one clause: where, time of day, weather or interior>
<light in one clause: source, direction, quality>
<one camera move, plain cinematography language>
<the action, last>
```

Example, 5 s:

```text
A barista in her thirties, black apron over a grey linen shirt, sleeves rolled.
A narrow espresso bar at dawn, steel counter, one window to the street.
Low sun through the window, warm side light, steam catching it.
Slow dolly push from a medium shot to a tight close-up on the cup.
She locks the portafilter, the first dark drops fall into the cup.
```

## Camera moves, one per shot

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| slow dolly push from medium shot to tight close-up | zoom in dramatically |
| locked-off static camera, no movement | no shaky camera |
| handheld tracking following the subject, subtle shake | dynamic follow cam |
| crane shot rising from ground level to overhead | epic aerial reveal |
| the camera glides alongside the subject as they move | smooth cinematic tracking |
| slow pan left to right across the room | sweeping pan |
| tilt up from the hands to the face | dramatic tilt |
| static wide shot, subject enters frame left | cinematic wide |

## Light and material words

Light: low sun, side light, top light, window light, overcast, tungsten
practicals, a single desk lamp, sodium street light, blue hour, dawn haze.

Material: linen, waxed cotton, brushed steel, wet asphalt, rough plaster, oak,
frosted glass, worn leather, cast iron, chalk.

Grade: neutral, warm, cool, desaturated, high contrast, lifted blacks, soft
highlights.

## Banned words in prompts

Never write these. Replace with camera, light and material language.

| Banned | Write instead |
|---|---|
| cinematic | name the move and the lens: "slow dolly push, shallow focus" |
| stunning, breathtaking, gorgeous, beautiful, amazing | the thing itself: "low sun on wet slate" |
| masterpiece | delete |
| 8k, 4k, ultra-realistic, highly detailed, photorealistic | delete; describe texture: "pores, stubble, linen weave" |
| epic | the scale: "crane shot rising to overhead, the valley opens" |
| dynamic, energetic | the motion: "she turns fast, the camera follows late" |

## Narration budget

About 2.3 words per second of clip. Count before you attach.

| Clip | Words |
|---|---|
| 5 s | 10 to 12 |
| 6 s | 13 to 14 |
| 8 s | 17 to 19 |
| 10 s | 20 to 24 |
| 12 s | 26 to 28 |

## Script register

Present tense. Concrete. One image per sentence. Never "in this video",
"imagine", "once upon a time", "in a world where", "welcome to".

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| Imagine a barista starting her day in a beautiful coffee bar. | Steam lifts off the portafilter. The first drops fall, dark and slow. |
| In this video we see a fox exploring a magical forest. | A fox stops at the tree line. Snow holds the last of the light. |
| Welcome to the most amazing sunrise you have ever seen. | The sun clears the ridge. The valley turns from grey to copper. |

## Music prompt

One bed for the whole short, instrumental, described as an arc:

```text
<instrument or texture>, opening on <a held state>, <one lift or turn> as <the action>, release into <a quiet ending>
```

Example: "solo piano and room tone, opening on a held chord, a small lift as
the cup fills, release into silence."
