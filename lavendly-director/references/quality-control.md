# Quality control checklist

Run it on the shot list before generation, on each scene's frame as it
lands, and on the rendered film. Not every line applies to every film.

## A. Story and value

- [ ] A clear purpose and audience.
- [ ] Scene 1 gives a reason to keep watching, inside two seconds.
- [ ] Each scene adds information, action, emotion or rhythm.
- [ ] The ending delivers the promise, the reveal or the next step.
- [ ] The visuals carry the message; nothing is decoration.

## B. Camera and composition

- [ ] Each shot has one subject and one visual priority.
- [ ] Shot size and angle suit the action; sizes change between neighbours.
- [ ] Every camera move has a reason; stillness where it adds nothing.
- [ ] The important action stays visible and readable.
- [ ] Foreground, background and negative space are intentional.
- [ ] Depth of field serves comprehension.

## C. Light and colour

- [ ] Light has a plausible or deliberately stylised source.
- [ ] Shadow direction and reflections agree, and hold within a scene.
- [ ] Important detail survives highlights and shadows.
- [ ] Materials react to light as they should.
- [ ] Colour fits the feeling and stays constant across the film.
- [ ] Grain, bloom, flare and haze only when asked.

## D. The hero and physical realism

- [ ] The hero is the same object or person in every frame: shape, material,
      colour, marks, wardrobe.
- [ ] Hands, tools and contact points are plausible.
- [ ] Every motion has a start state and an end state.
- [ ] Nothing floats, slides or changes shape without a reason.
- [ ] Background geometry and object positions hold.
- [ ] No phone UI, timestamp, recorder dot, watermark or stray text.
- [ ] Any text, logo or label was opened and read, or left blank on purpose.

## E. Continuity and the cut

- [ ] Screen direction and geography are understandable.
- [ ] Actions connect across cuts; a continuing shot starts where the last
      one ended.
- [ ] Each shot has time to register; the total matches the target.
- [ ] Transitions are hard cuts or dips to black chosen for meaning.
- [ ] Trims keep the strongest window of each clip.

## F. Sound

- [ ] Narration and dialogue intelligible, inside the word budget.
- [ ] Foley matches the action; ambience holds within a place.
- [ ] One score, with an arc, ducked under the voice.
- [ ] No clipping, abrupt cutoff, silence by accident, or black padding.

## G. Workflow and honesty

- [ ] Every operation used exists in the MCP catalog.
- [ ] Still-frame prompts are separate from motion prompts.
- [ ] Complex actions are split into clips.
- [ ] The hero reference is wired into every scene.
- [ ] Scene 1 was checked before scene 2 was paid for.
- [ ] Known limits were said plainly (no dissolves, no rendered titles).
- [ ] Nothing was reported as checked unless a frame was opened.

## Approval rule

Approve a shot when it serves the story, fits the sequence and the hero is
itself, not because it looks attractive on its own.
