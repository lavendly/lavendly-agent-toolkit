# Recipe: day in the life

A day, a routine or a vlog compressed into quick, lived-in moments from
morning to night. Worked example: a day in the life of Ana, a fictional
ceramicist with a small studio. The user's own subject replaces her.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: intimacy and rhythm: the viewer feels a day in 30 seconds and
  wants the next one.
- Audience: followers who come back for the person; vertical, sound on,
  often watched twice.
- The ending is the day's last small moment (the light off, the kiln
  ticking, a mug in bed), with one line that makes tomorrow a promise.

## 2. Format rules

- Chronological, time of day spoken, never drawn ("six a.m.", "lunch",
  "nine p.m."). Light changes with the time; every jump in time is a new
  heading in the plan and the light line changes with it.
- Short shots: 1.5 to 3 s each, generated at 4 or 5 s and trimmed. One
  small action per shot, mostly hands and objects, the face in a few.
- Phone-footage realism (phone-footage.md): Ana films herself, phone
  propped or at arm's length, a current phone; one imperfection per shot.
- One person, the same in every shot: a face close-up reference and an
  outfit reference on a 4-reference model; her description verbatim.
- If the user wants their own real day, their footage is the spine
  (import) and generated shots only fill gaps (objects, places), never the
  user's face.

### 60 s

Two of the 30 s days back to back is boring; instead the 30 s plan with a
middle story beat (a customer order, a delivery, a friend dropping by seen
from behind) and one longer held moment (8 s at the kiln at night). About
22 clips, about 120 words. Offer 30 s first.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the most intimate or surprising first moment with a spoken time
  ("Five a.m., and the kiln is still warm."). Or the day's end first, then
  "Let me show you how I got here."
- Payoff: the day's result (the bowl out of the kiln, the delivered order,
  the quiet evening), and a line that promises the next one.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- "Phone video, phone propped on a shelf" for wide self-filmed moments;
  "filmed at arm's length on a phone front camera" for face moments;
  "phone rear camera, handheld" for hands.
- Light changes with the hour, written in each heading: dawn "cool blue
  window light, warm lamp", midday "bright overcast skylight", dusk "low
  orange sun through dusty glass", night "one warm bulb, the kiln's
  orange glow".
- Colour: "colour straight from the phone, warm indoors, cool outdoors";
  the studio palette anchors: "grey clay, cream sweater, terracotta,
  pale wood".
- Pacing: 2 to 3 s average; one held 4 to 8 s moment at the emotional
  centre.

## 6. Sound

- Voice: diary register, first person, present tense, 2.5 words per second
  at most; jessica, lily or liam. 15 s about 25 words, 30 s about 60.
- Native sound is half the format: the wheel's hum, wet clay, a bike bell,
  the kiln ticking. Clips `mix` 0.4 under the voice, `native` where no line
  plays.
- Bed: "Lo-fi warm keys and soft vinyl crackle at about 85 BPM, gentle and
  constant, a short stop on the collapse, back in on the second attempt."
  Volume 0.25, ducking on.
- Captions: `reels` (minimal) or `tiktok`.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Seedance 2.0 Mini)

References: reference 1 Ana's face close-up on grey, reference 2 her apron
and sweater. Role sentence in every motion prompt.

**Pair 1, the wheel.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Phone video still, phone rear camera, handheld. Ana's hands, slim, clay-streaked, cuffs of an oversized cream sweater pushed up, cup a wet lump of grey clay centred on a spinning pottery wheel head. Close-up from above at a slight angle, phone wide lens. Cool blue dawn light from a window on the left, one warm lamp. Splashed wheel tray, a sponge, a wooden rib tool. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Phone video, unstabilised handheld phone camera. Reference 1 is Ana's face only; reference 2 is her sweater and apron only; ignore their backgrounds. The wheel spins and her thumbs press into the centre of the clay, opening it, already in motion from the first frame; a little water flicks off the edge. The camera sways slightly, focus pulsing once. Ends with a clean round opening in the clay. Exactly two hands, no other person. Sound: wheel motor hum, wet clay. No music. Clean frame, no text, no recording dot, no timestamp.
```

**Pair 2, the collapse.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Phone video still, phone propped on a shelf. Ana, a woman in her late twenties with a messy dark bun, a clay-stained grey linen apron over an oversized cream sweater, sits at a pottery wheel, hands on a tall thin wet clay cylinder, looking at it, not at the camera. Medium shot, phone wide lens, slightly tilted. Bright overcast skylight. Shelves of bisque bowls behind. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
STARTING STATE: Ana at the wheel, hands on a tall wet cylinder, phone propped, frame slightly tilted. TIMELINE: [0-2s] the cylinder's wall wobbles under her fingers, already in motion from the first frame. [2-4s] it folds and slumps sideways into a flat heap; she lifts both hands away and laughs. CAMERA: static propped phone, horizon a few degrees off. AUDIO: wheel hum slowing, a wet slump, her short laugh, no music. ENDING STATE: the collapsed clay, Ana laughing, hands raised. Exactly one person.
```

**Second family, Veo 3.1 Fast.** Same content in the five-part order,
`Ambient noise: wheel motor hum` and `SFX: a wet slump`, durations 4 s;
two references at most (face first).

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Ana's face changes between shots | Face reference with its role sentence; the character block verbatim; more hands-only shots |
| Clay physics wrong (clay floating, wheel spinning backwards) | One action, "the wheel spins counter-clockwise", start and end states |
| A second person appears | "Exactly one person"; for friends, an introduced figure seen from behind |
| The phone shows a readable screen | "phone face down", "screen turned away" |
| Every shot the same light | A light line per time heading |
| Too slick | Phone defects and one imperfection per shot |

## 10. Policy guards

- A generated day is fiction: never present it as a real person's real
  life; the post caption says AI-generated. Real days use the user's own
  footage.
- No real person's face; no real brand logos in the set.
- No minors as the vlogger in realistic phone footage.
- Product placements in the day follow the platform's paid-partnership
  disclosure rules; the user turns that on when posting.
