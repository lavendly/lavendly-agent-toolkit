# Recipe: POV

"POV: you are ..." puts the viewer inside a situation: the camera is their
eyes, their hands are at the bottom of the frame, other people talk to the
lens. Worked example: POV, your first morning at a bakery and the oven is
already smoking; the user's own situation replaces it. FPV flight is covered in section 9.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: a relatable or absurd situation lived in first person, with an
  escalation and a punchline.
- Audience: TikTok, Reels, Shorts viewers who know the format and decide in
  one second.
- The ending is the punchline: one last look, one last line, or a cut to
  black on the reaction. Loops well when the last line leads into the
  first.

## 2. Format rules

- Camera at head height, moving like a head: small turns, a glance down at
  the hands, never a gimbal glide. "First-person view, head-height, hands
  at the bottom of the frame."
- The viewer's hands are the hero: lock them in one block (sleeves, a ring,
  a watch) and paste it into every still prompt.
- People in the scene look into the lens when they speak to "you"; one
  speaker per clip, line spoken natively (Veo 3.1 Fast, Kling v3), one
  sentence of 12 words at most in an 8 s clip.
- "POV:" is spoken as the first word of the voiceover so the captions show
  it; there is no on-screen text.
- Continuity: the hands, the place and the light carry through the scene;
  a continuous walk chains each opening frame from the previous last frame
  (two or three hops, then re-anchor).

### 60 s

A POV mini-story in four scenes of 15 s (arrival, first disaster, the
rush, the redemption), each scene built like the 15 s plan, one native line
per scene, the voice only on the hook and one mid-point line. About 20
clips; 60 s rarely beats 30 s in this format, so offer 30 s first.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: "POV:" and the situation in one breath, over the most absurd frame
  (smoke rolling out of a door at you).
- Payoff: the reaction line to the lens, then one frame of silence. The
  joke lands on the cut.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- "First-person view, head-height, hands at the bottom of the frame,
  natural head sway, phone wide lens."
- Head moves are glances (tilt down to the hands, a quick pan to a sound);
  one per clip. No orbit, no crane.
- Light as the place has it: "fluorescent tubes and smoky window light";
  phone colour, a little warm indoors.
- One imperfection per shot from phone-footage.md (exposure swing at the
  window, focus hunt on the tray).

## 6. Sound

- Voice: only the hook and, in long versions, one mid line; laura, jessica
  or will; 2.5 words per second.
- Native sound carries the joke: alarm chirp, oven hiss, a bell; clips
  `native` at 1, or `mix` 0.5 under the voice line.
- Lines spoken to the lens in the clip itself (native).
- Bed: none, or a light bed at 0.2: "Bright pizzicato and a light kick at
  about 115 BPM, stops dead one beat before the punchline line."
- Captions: `tiktok` on the voiceover; spoken lines in clips rely on the
  platform's auto-captions.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Veo 3.1 Fast)

**Pair 1, the hook.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
First-person view, head-height, hands at the bottom of the frame. Your hands, slim, flour-dusted white apron sleeves at each wrist, a thin silver ring on the left index finger, press flat against a scuffed green back door of a bakery, the door just opening. Phone wide lens, slight barrel distortion. Grey morning light through the gap. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
First-person view, head-height, one continuous take, unstabilised handheld like a head moving. 0 to 1 s: the hands push the door fully open, already in motion from the first frame. 1 to 3 s: grey smoke rolls out of an open oven at the far end of a small kitchen and drifts toward the camera; the view stops in the doorway. 3 to 4 s: the head tilts slightly as the smoke thickens. Camera: a short push forward through the door, stopping on the threshold. End state: smoke filling the upper half of the frame, the oven glowing orange behind it. The hands, the kitchen and the light stay as in the opening frame. Exactly two hands, nobody else in frame. SFX: a smoke alarm chirps twice. No music. Clean frame: no text, no recording dot, no timestamp, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Pair 2, the line to the lens.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Phone video still, handheld. Marta, a head baker in her fifties with grey hair in a tight bun, a flour-streaked navy apron and reading glasses pushed up on her head, stands in a smoky bakery kitchen holding a tray of burnt black croissants, looking straight into the lens. Medium close-up, phone wide lens, her face in the upper middle of the frame. Fluorescent light and grey smoke. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Phone video, one continuous take, handheld with slight sway. Medium close-up. 0 to 2 s: Marta holds the tray still and stares into the lens. 2 to 5 s: she says, flat and tired: "Those were the customer's." (no subtitles) 5 to 8 s: she lowers the tray slowly and keeps staring. Camera: locked to her face, small natural sway. End state: tray lowered, the stare held. Marta, the kitchen and the smoke stay as in the opening frame. Exactly one person in frame. Ambient noise: oven fan hum. No music. Clean frame: no text, no recording dot, no timestamp, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Second family, Seedance 2.0 Mini.**

```text
STARTING STATE: first-person view at head height, two hands pressing open a green back door into a smoky bakery kitchen. TIMELINE: [0-2s] the door swings fully open, already in motion from the first frame; smoke rolls toward the camera. [2-4s] the view stops in the doorway. CAMERA: head-height push forward, stops on the threshold, natural sway. AUDIO: two smoke alarm chirps, oven hum, no music. ENDING STATE: smoke in the upper half of the frame, the oven glowing behind.
```

Seedance has no quoted speech budget worth relying on; put spoken lines on
Veo 3.1 Fast clips in a mixed film only if the look matches, otherwise keep
lines in the voiceover.

## 9. FPV and action POV

- "FPV drone view, very wide lens, fast and low, banking on turns": one
  segment per clip under 6 s, a gap or doorway as the hook, a slowdown at
  the reveal. Chain segments with the last frame as the next opening image
  (`/api` plus the path `get_scene_frame` returns; never from a trimmed end);
  rooms rearrange themselves after two or three hops, so cut to a new space
  instead of chaining further.
- Sound: wind rush or motor whine native; a bed of 120 to 140 BPM.
- Kling v3 Pro holds fast motion best; Veo 3.1 Fast holds places best.

## 10. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| A body or face appears in "your" view | "Exactly two hands, nobody else in frame"; the camera at head height |
| A third hand | The hands block names two hands and what each holds |
| The camera glides like a gimbal | "unstabilised handheld like a head moving" |
| The person speaking looks off-lens | "looking straight into the lens" in both prompts |
| The line is mumbled or out of sync | One short sentence, medium close-up, locked; re-roll once |
| A recording dot or timestamp | The phone clean-frame line |
| The place changes between shots | Name the same 6 to 8 objects in every still prompt of the scene |

## 11. Policy guards

- Characters are fictional; no real person's face, no real business's
  name or logo in the set.
- A POV that depicts real professions or places stays a comedy sketch, not
  a claim about a real workplace.
- No minors as the speaking character in realistic phone footage.
- The post caption says the video is AI-generated.
