# Recipe: watercolour and storybook

The picture-book look: watercolour washes, gouache, ink and wash, coloured
pencil and crayon on textured paper, moving gently like a page come alive.
This recipe owns the look; [recipe-kids-fables.md](recipe-kids-fables.md)
owns the story, the pacing for children and the safety rules.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Picture-book stories, gentle brand films, poems, memorial and family films,
seasonal greetings. All ages, mostly calm viewing. The ending is a still
page: the last image settles, the paint stops moving, the frame holds for
2 s or more like a closing spread.

## 2. Structure by length

Built on Wan 2.7 (whole lengths 2 to 10 s; 5 s is one block and a
picture-book pace). Worked example: Nilo, a young river hippo who is afraid
of deep water, on a reed-lined river; the user's own story replaces it.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): the page is already alive: a wash spreading, ripples
  on the water, ears turning toward a sound; a question in the narration.
- Payoff: one small action that answers it, then the paint settles and the
  page holds.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: locked-off pages and slow pans that read a spread from left to
  right, slow push-ins for feeling. No orbit, no handheld, no fast move: a
  turning camera exposes the flat art and invites 3D drift.
- Media, as words:
  - Watercolour: "wet-edged washes, pigment pooling darker at the edges,
    soft blooms where wet paint meets wet paint, granulation in the
    shadows, the white of the paper as the highlight, visible cold-press
    grain".
  - Gouache: "matte opaque paint, flat shapes with soft brush edges, chalky
    light colours".
  - Ink and wash: "a loose dip-pen ink line with watercolour washes inside".
  - Coloured pencil and crayon: "visible pencil hatching", "waxy crayon
    strokes on toothy paper".
- Motion: slow, gentle, held; transitions inside a shot are washes ("a
  wash of lavender spreads across the sky as dusk comes").
- Light: painted, not rendered: warm and cool areas of wash, the paper white
  for low sun on the water and the stars.
- Colour: 4 to 5 colours from the bible, soft and slightly desaturated; the
  darkest dark is a deep wash, never black.
- Exact phrases: "Watercolour storybook animation, wet-edged washes,
  visible cold-press paper grain, soft pencil underdrawing", "Gouache
  storybook animation, matte opaque paint with soft brush edges on textured
  paper", "the paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves", "slow,
  gentle motion, long holds".

## 5. Sound

- Native: soft ambience (crickets, wind in the reeds, a soft lap of water),
  `mix` at 0.3 to 0.4 under narration.
- Narration: the storyteller carries it: 15 s about 30 words, 30 s about 60,
  60 s about 110, at 2.0 to 2.1 words per second.
- Score brief: "Celesta, harp and felt piano at about 70 BPM, with a solo
  cello entering at the turn; soft and unhurried, it slows and resolves on a
  single held harp chord on the last page."
- Mix: score 0.25 to 0.3 with `ducking: true`; no SFX louder than the
  narration.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Wan dialect)

Pair 1: the first step (the 6 s page)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Watercolour storybook animation, wet-edged washes, visible cold-press paper grain, soft pencil underdrawing. Nilo: a young round grey-lavender river hippo on four short legs, a wide pink muzzle, small round ears, small dark eyes, a yellow knotted neckerchief, a pale pink patch over the left eye only. Nilo stands on the muddy bank of a reed-lined river at dusk, the water still. Wide shot, eye level. Low warm light from the left. Palette: reed gold, cream, lavender, river green. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Watercolour storybook animation, slow gentle motion, long holds. 0 to 2 s: Nilo lowers his head and looks at the deep water, his ears flat. 2 to 4 s: he lifts one front foot and touches it to the surface; soft rings of ripples spread across the river. 4 to 6 s: the foot rests in the shallows, his ears twitch once, and he holds still. Camera: slow push-in, stopping at a medium shot. End state: Nilo on the bank on all four legs, one front foot in the shallows, the ripples fading. The paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves; the river and the reeds stay as in the opening frame; the water is a flat wash with no mirror reflection. Exactly one hippo in frame. Sound: crickets, a soft lap of water. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the dragonfly (gouache)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Gouache storybook animation, matte opaque paint with soft brush edges on textured paper. Nilo: a young round grey-lavender river hippo on four short legs, a wide pink muzzle, small round ears, small dark eyes, a yellow knotted neckerchief, a pale pink patch over the left eye only. Nilo stands in the shallows between tall reeds, looking up at a pale empty sky. Medium shot, eye level. Soft morning light. Palette: reed gold, cream, slate blue, river green. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Gouache storybook animation, slow gentle motion. 0 to 3 s: the one dragonfly, the only one in the air, drifts down from the top of frame in front of Nilo's face. 3 to 5 s: it lands on the tip of his nose; his eyes cross to look at it. 5 to 6 s: he holds still, a small surprised smile, mouth closed. Camera: locked-off. End state: the dragonfly resting on his nose. The matte paint texture stays fixed; the reeds and the water stay as in the opening frame. Exactly one hippo and one dragonfly. Sound: a hush of wind in the reeds. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Veo 3.1 Fast), pair 1 in Veo order: "Medium-wide shot,
eye level. Nilo, the young grey-lavender river hippo with the yellow
neckerchief, stands on the river bank at dusk. He lowers his head and
touches one front foot to the water; ripples spread; his ears twitch.
Watercolour storybook animation, the paper texture fixed, slow push-in.
Ambient noise: crickets, lapping water. No music. Clean frame: no text, no
on-screen graphics."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The paint crawls or boils over the whole frame | "the paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves"; lower motion; shorter clip |
| Drifts into glossy digital painting or 3D | the medium words verbatim in both prompts; the plate as reference; no camera orbit |
| Edges turn crisp and vector-like | "wet-edged washes, soft blooms, pigment pooling at the edges" |
| Colours oversaturate | "soft, slightly desaturated washes"; the bible palette only |
| Pencil line disappears or doubles | "soft pencil underdrawing" in the style line of every prompt |
| Black shadows appear | "the darkest dark is a deep indigo wash" |
| Dragonflies, stars or fireflies multiply | "the one dragonfly, the only one in the air" |

## 9. Policy guards

- No illustrator, picture-book or studio name; describe the media and the
  palette.
- No existing picture-book characters or a design close to one.
- A film for children also follows the safety rules in
  [recipe-kids-fables.md](recipe-kids-fables.md).
