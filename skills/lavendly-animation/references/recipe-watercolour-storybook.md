# Recipe: watercolour and storybook

The picture-book look: watercolour washes, gouache, ink and wash, coloured
pencil and crayon on textured paper, moving gently like a page come alive.
This recipe owns the look; [recipe-kids-fables.md](recipe-kids-fables.md)
owns the story, the pacing for children and the safety rules.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Picture-book stories, gentle brand films, poems, memorial and family films,
seasonal greetings. All ages, mostly calm viewing. The ending is a still
page: the last image settles, the paint stops moving, the frame holds for
2 s or more like a closing spread.

## 2. Structure by length

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): the page is already alive: a wash spreading, a lantern
  flickering, ears turning toward a sound; a question in the narration.
- Payoff: one small action that answers it, then the paint settles and the
  page holds.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: locked-off pages and slow pans that read a spread from left to
  right, slow push-ins for feeling. No orbit, no handheld, no fast move: a
  turning camera exposes the flat art and invites 3D drift.
- Media, as words:
  - Watercolour: "wet-edged washes, pigment pooling darker at the edges,
    soft blooms where wet paint meets wet paint, granulation in the
    shadows, the white of the paper as the highlight, visible cold-press
    grain".
  - Gouache: "matte opaque paint, flat shapes with soft brush edges, chalky
    light colours".
  - Ink and wash: "a loose dip-pen ink line with watercolour washes inside".
  - Coloured pencil and crayon: "visible pencil hatching", "waxy crayon
    strokes on toothy paper".
- Motion: slow, gentle, held; transitions inside a shot are washes ("a
  wash of lavender spreads across the sky as dusk comes").
- Light: painted, not rendered: warm and cool areas of wash, the paper white
  for the lantern glow and the stars.
- Colour: 4 to 5 colours from the bible, soft and slightly desaturated; the
  darkest dark is a deep wash, never black.
- Exact phrases: "Watercolour storybook animation, wet-edged washes,
  visible cold-press paper grain, soft pencil underdrawing", "Gouache
  storybook animation, matte opaque paint with soft brush edges on textured
  paper", "the paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves", "slow,
  gentle motion, long holds".

## 5. Sound

- Native: soft ambience (crickets, wind in pines, a crackle of the lantern),
  `mix` at 0.3 to 0.4 under narration.
- Narration: the storyteller carries it: 15 s about 30 words, 30 s about 60,
  60 s about 110, at 2.0 to 2.1 words per second.
- Score brief: "Celesta, harp and felt piano at about 70 BPM, with a solo
  cello entering at the turn; soft and unhurried, it slows and resolves on a
  single held harp chord on the last page."
- Mix: score 0.25 to 0.3 with `ducking: true`; no SFX louder than the
  narration.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Wan dialect)

Pair 1: the first star (the 6 s page)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Watercolour storybook animation, wet-edged washes, visible cold-press paper grain, soft pencil underdrawing. Pip: a small round red fox cub on four legs, oversized pointed ears, cream chest and muzzle, black socks on all four paws, large amber eyes, a blue knitted scarf, a white tip on the left ear only. Pip sits at the forest edge at dusk, an unlit paper lantern at his paws. Wide shot, eye level. Low warm light from the left. Palette: rust red, cream, lavender, pine green. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Watercolour storybook animation, slow gentle motion, long holds. 0 to 2 s: Pip nudges the paper lantern upright with his nose and it begins to glow warm from inside. 2 to 4 s: a lavender wash deepens across the sky and one small star appears above the pines. 4 to 6 s: Pip looks up at it, his ears twitch once, and he holds still. Camera: slow push-in, stopping at a medium shot. End state: Pip seated, looking up at the one star, the lantern glowing. The paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves; the forest stays as in the opening frame. Exactly one fox cub, one lantern and one star. Sound: crickets, a soft paper crackle. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the first snowflake (gouache)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Gouache storybook animation, matte opaque paint with soft brush edges on textured paper. Pip: a small round red fox cub on four legs, oversized pointed ears, cream chest and muzzle, black socks on all four paws, large amber eyes, a blue knitted scarf, a white tip on the left ear only. Pip sits in a snowy clearing between pine trunks, looking up at a pale empty sky. Medium shot, eye level. Soft winter light. Palette: rust red, cream, slate blue, pine green. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Gouache storybook animation, slow gentle motion. 0 to 3 s: the one snowflake, the only one in the air, drifts down from the top of frame in front of Pip's face. 3 to 5 s: it lands on the tip of his nose; his eyes cross to look at it. 5 to 6 s: he holds still, a small surprised smile, mouth closed. Camera: locked-off. End state: the snowflake resting on his nose. The matte paint texture stays fixed; the clearing stays as in the opening frame. Exactly one fox cub and one snowflake. Sound: a hush of wind in the pines. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Veo 3.1 Fast), pair 1 in Veo order: "Medium-wide shot,
eye level. Pip, the red fox cub with the blue scarf, sits at the forest edge
at dusk. He nudges the paper lantern upright and it glows; a star appears
above the pines; he looks up and his ears twitch. Watercolour storybook
animation, the paper texture fixed, slow push-in. Ambient noise: crickets.
No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The paint crawls or boils over the whole frame | "the paper texture stays fixed while the paint moves"; lower motion; shorter clip |
| Drifts into glossy digital painting or 3D | the medium words verbatim in both prompts; the plate as reference; no camera orbit |
| Edges turn crisp and vector-like | "wet-edged washes, soft blooms, pigment pooling at the edges" |
| Colours oversaturate | "soft, slightly desaturated washes"; the bible palette only |
| Pencil line disappears or doubles | "soft pencil underdrawing" in the style line of every prompt |
| Black shadows appear | "the darkest dark is a deep indigo wash" |
| Snow, stars or fireflies multiply | "the one snowflake, the only one in the air" |

## 9. Policy guards

- No illustrator, picture-book or studio name; describe the media and the
  palette.
- No existing picture-book characters or a design close to one.
- A film for children also follows the safety rules in
  [recipe-kids-fables.md](recipe-kids-fables.md).
