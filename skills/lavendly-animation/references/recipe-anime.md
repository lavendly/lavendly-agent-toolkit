# Recipe: anime

Japanese-style 2D cel animation described by its craft (line, cel shading,
painted backgrounds, limited animation, framing), never by a studio, series
or character name.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Slice-of-life moments, stylised drama, action bursts, music visualisers and
channel intros for teens and adults who know the form. The ending is either
an emotional close-up that holds (eyes, a breath, wind in the hair) or a
quiet "pillow shot" (sky, a train, power lines) that lets the feeling land
after the action.

## 2. Structure by length

Built on Wan 2.7 (any whole length from 2 to 10 s; up to 5 s is one block,
so short anime holds are cheap). Example: Rin on the school rooftop at
sunset.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 1 s): a key-frame composition that could be a poster: hair in
  wind against a huge sky, an extreme close-up on eyes, or a speed-line burst
  with an impact sound on frame 1.
- Payoff: one held close-up after the motion stops, or a pillow shot that
  lets the silence ring. In action, impact first, then 2 to 4 s of quiet.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Limited-animation camera: slow pans across still painted backgrounds,
  tilts from feet to face, slow push-ins on eyes, low-angle hero frames,
  a Dutch angle for unease, camera shake only on impact.
- Framing conventions: characters small against big skies; pillow shots of
  objects and places between scenes (power lines, trains, vending machine
  glow, crossing gates, clouds); extreme close-ups of eyes and hands;
  over-the-shoulder two-shots.
- Line and shading: heavier outer contour, thinner inner lines, two-tone
  hard-edged cel shadows, highlight bands on hair, large eyes with two
  catchlights; backgrounds painted in more detail than the characters.
- Light: rim glints on hair, sunset backlight with long warm shadows, cool
  blue night with warm windows, god rays through clouds when motivated.
- Colour: saturated sky blue, towering white cumulus, sunset orange to rose;
  night in navy and teal with warm sodium-orange accents.
- Exact phrases: "2D cel-shaded animation, clean line art with a heavier
  outer contour, two-tone hard shadows, hand-painted backgrounds", "flat 2D
  cel shading, the drawing stays flat", "limited animation on twos",
  "speed lines behind him", "painted cumulus clouds", "hair moving in
  separate strands".

## 5. Sound

- Native: exaggerated SFX (whoosh, impact, a chime on a realisation), wind,
  rain, cicadas, train bells. Action clips `native` at 1.
- Narration: an inner monologue, short and sparse: 15 s up to 25 words, 30 s
  up to 50, 60 s up to 100. One voice; English or Japanese (the ElevenLabs
  multilingual models speak Japanese).
- Dialogue: one short line per clip, mouth flaps suit the style; medium
  close-up, one speaking face.
- Score brief, drama: "Solo piano at about 80 BPM, strings enter at the turn,
  held chord on the final close-up; instrumental." Action: "Taiko, synth bass
  and strings at about 140 BPM, a hit on the catch, then a hard stop into
  the quiet coda with solo piano."
- Mix: SFX-led clips `native`; monologue clips `mix` at 0.4; score 0.35
  with `ducking: true` under the voice; a music edit sets clips `off` and the
  score at 0.9 to 1.

Seedance and Wan bill each clip of 5 s or less as one block, so the 4-shot
15 s cut is 4 blocks. Seedance makes nothing under 4 s: generate the 3 s
shots at 4 s and trim (same price). The 60 s cut is 13 clips: 180 credits
on Wan 2.7 (eleven one-block clips and two 6 s clips at 2 blocks each),
plus 13 opening images.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Wan dialect)

Pair 1: rooftop at sunset (the 6 s moment)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
2D cel-shaded animation, clean line art with a heavier outer contour, two-tone hard shadows, hand-painted backgrounds. Rin: a girl of about sixteen, short black bob, one white hair clip on the right side, grey eyes, navy sailor-collar school uniform with a red neckerchief. Rin stands at a rooftop railing, seen from behind, towering orange clouds over a low town. Low angle, wide lens. Hard rim light from the setting sun. Palette: sunset orange, navy, rose, pale gold. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
2D cel-shaded animation, limited animation on twos. 0 to 2 s: wind lifts Rin's hair in separate strands and ripples her neckerchief; the clouds drift slowly left. 2 to 6 s: she turns her head over her right shoulder toward camera and her eyes catch the light. Camera: slow tilt up from the railing to her face, stopping at a medium close-up. End state: Rin looking back over her shoulder, hair settling. Flat 2D cel shading, the drawing stays flat; the rooftop and the sky stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one girl in frame, nobody else. Sound: wind over the rooftop, a distant crossing bell. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the catch (shot 7 of the 60 s cut)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
2D cel-shaded animation, clean line art with a heavier outer contour, two-tone hard shadows, hand-painted backgrounds. Taro: a boy of about fifteen, messy brown hair, a yellow hooded raincoat, white sneakers. Taro mid-dive, low over a wet crosswalk at night, one hand reaching for a single orange in the air. Medium close-up, low angle. Cool blue street light, warm orange reflections on the wet road. Palette: navy, teal, sodium orange, white. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
2D cel-shaded animation, limited animation on twos. 0 to 1 s: speed lines streak behind Taro as he lunges forward. 1 to 2 s: his hand closes around the one orange; camera shake on the catch, a burst of rain droplets. 2 to 4 s: he lands on his knees on the wet crosswalk, clutching the orange to his chest, and holds still. Camera: locked-off apart from the shake. End state: Taro kneeling, the orange held safe. Flat 2D cel shading, the drawing stays flat. Exactly one boy and one orange in frame, nobody else. Sound: a whoosh, a soft impact thump, rain hiss. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Kling): scene, characters, action, camera, audio, style, in
plain verbs. Pair 1 as Kling: "A school rooftop at sunset. Rin, the girl
with the black bob and white hair clip, stands at the railing. The wind
lifts her hair; she turns her head over her right shoulder toward the
camera. Slow tilt up from the railing to her face. Wind, a distant crossing
bell. 2D cel-shaded animation, flat cel shading, limited animation on twos.
Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Slides into 3D or semi-real shading | "flat 2D cel shading, the drawing stays flat" in the lock sentence; the plate as reference; shorter clips |
| Line art boils, faces shift during a head turn | limited animation: the body holds still while one part moves; at most one head turn per clip |
| Eyes change size or colour between shots | eye shape and colour in the hero block; the head close-up as reference 1 |
| Petals, sparkles and lens flares everywhere | name one particle or none; "the one orange is the only thing in the air" |
| Japanese signs become gibberish characters | signs glow blank or turn away; "blank glowing signs" |
| Hair changes length or colour in wind | hair length and colour in the hero block; "hair moving in strands, same length" |
| Mouth moves while the monologue plays | "mouth closed" in narrated shots |
| Speed lines turn into streaks of noise | speed lines only in the shot that needs them, under 2 s |

## 9. Policy guards

- No studio, series, director or character name. No existing character
  designs, uniforms, emblems or signature props from a known series.
- Characters of school age are written and framed as young people in
  everyday situations; never suggestive framing or clothing.
- No real people drawn in anime style without consent.
