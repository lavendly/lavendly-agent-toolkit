# Recipe: paper cutout and collage

Two related looks. Cut paper: layered card with thickness, torn edges, small
shadows between layers, pieces that slide and rotate on hinged joints.
Collage: halftone cut-out photographs, kraft paper, newsprint, tape and
scissor edges, the editorial explainer look. Both are born in the still and
move in simple, stepped ways, which suits video models well.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Fables, explainers, editorial essays, brand stories, music visualisers for
adults and children. The ending is a composed tableau that settles: every
piece slides into its final place and the frame holds like a finished page.

Collage variant (an editorial explainer): the same lengths, each shot a
new cut-out arriving on kraft paper under narration, a match on shape
between neighbours (a round clock face, then a round coffee cup, then the
sun).

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): a piece already moving on a textured ground (a paper
  wave rising, a cut-out photo sliding in and stopping with a small bounce),
  with a paper sound on frame 1.
- Payoff: the final tableau assembles itself, piece by piece, and holds.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- The rostrum camera: straight-on or straight-down over flat artwork.
  Moves are pans and slow push-ins across the artwork, and trucks where
  the layers move at different speeds (foreground fastest), which gives
  the multiplane depth. No orbit, no tilt that reveals the artwork is flat
  unless that is the joke.
- Motion: pieces slide, rotate on hinged joints (split pins at elbows and
  knees), swap for replacement pieces (a mouth piece, an eye piece);
  stepped at about 8 to 12 frames per second.
- Light: one soft directional light raking across the layers so each casts
  a small soft shadow on the one below; the paper texture fixed to the frame.
- Colour: paper colours with a printed matte quality; cut paper in 4 to 5
  colours on a textured ground; collage in cream, black, one strong red and
  halftone greys.
- Exact phrases: "Cut-paper stop-motion animation, layered card with visible
  thickness, torn white fibre edges, small shadows between layers", "Paper
  collage animation, halftone cut-out photos, kraft paper and newsprint,
  tape and torn edges", "pieces slide and rotate on hinged joints, stepped
  cut-out animation", "the paper texture stays fixed; only the pieces move".

## 5. Sound

- Native: paper rustle, a card slide, a scissor snip, tape tearing, a soft
  tap as a piece lands. Clips `native` at 1, or `mix` at 0.4 under narration.
- Narration: the collage explainer is narration-led: 15 s about 35 words,
  30 s about 70, 60 s about 140, at 2.3 to 2.5 words per second; a fable
  at 2.0.
- Score brief, fable: "Marimba, plucked strings and a music box at about 95
  BPM; calm at first, the strings tremble through the storm, a single
  music-box phrase when the moon returns, a warm resolved chord in the
  harbour." Editorial: "Upright bass, brushed drums and a muted piano at
  about 100 BPM, light and curious, a small button at the end."
- Mix: score 0.3 to 0.35 with `ducking: true` under narration.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Kling dialect)

Pair 1: the storm (shot 3 of the 30 s cut)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Cut-paper stop-motion animation, layered card with visible thickness, torn white fibre edges, small shadows between layers. Skiff: a small paper fishing boat, red hull, cream paper sail on a split-pin mast, a tiny round porthole. Skiff sits between three rows of torn navy and teal paper waves under a dark grey paper sky. Wide shot, straight-on, flat artwork, deep focus. Soft raking light from the upper left. Palette: navy, teal, red, cream, slate. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Scene: a cut-paper sea in a storm. Character: Skiff, the small red paper boat. Action: the three rows of torn-paper waves rise and fall in turn, each layer sliding on its own; Skiff is tossed up on the middle row and tips left, then right, then settles in a trough. Camera: locked-off, straight-on. Audio: paper rustle, card sliding on card, a low wind. Style: stepped cut-out animation at about 8 frames per second; pieces slide and rotate; the paper texture stays fixed and only the pieces move. Exactly one boat, nobody else. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the editorial collage (a 5 s explainer beat)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Paper collage animation, halftone cut-out photos, kraft paper and newsprint, tape and torn edges. A halftone cut-out photo of a round vintage alarm clock with a white scissor-cut border, lying on brown kraft paper, one strip of torn masking tape across its top corner, a red paper sun cut-out at the right edge. Top-down, flat, deep focus. Soft daylight from the left, small shadows under each cut-out. Palette: kraft brown, cream, black, red. No text, no letters, blank clock face.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Scene: a kraft paper table in a collage. Action: the cut-out alarm clock rattles in small stepped jumps, then slides left out of frame; the red paper sun slides in from the right and stops at centre with a small bounce. Camera: locked-off, top-down. Audio: a paper rattle, a soft slide, a light tap. Style: paper collage animation, stepped at about 10 frames per second; the kraft paper and the tape stay fixed. Exactly one clock and one sun. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Runway, motion only, silent): "The torn-paper waves rise and
fall in layers; the paper boat tips left and right and settles in a trough.
Locked-off camera, stepped cut-out motion, the paper texture stays still."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Paper bends like cloth or turns into 3D | "rigid card pieces slide and rotate; nothing bends"; flat straight-on camera |
| Layers merge, shadows vanish | "small soft shadows between layers" in both prompts; raking light named |
| Texture crawls during motion | "the paper texture stays fixed; only the pieces move" |
| Halftone photos become realistic footage | "halftone cut-out photo with a white scissor-cut border" in the still; stepped motion |
| Newsprint and clock faces sprout letters | blank clock faces, blank newsprint areas, "no text, no letters" |
| Smooth motion breaks the look | "stepped at about 8 to 12 frames per second" second in the prompt |
| Pieces fly in from nowhere | say where each piece enters and where it stops |

## 9. Policy guards

- Collage photos are invented, never real photographs of real people, real
  news events or brand packaging, and never a real newspaper's masthead or
  page.
- No editorial look attributed to a named publication or channel; describe
  the materials.
- An explainer that states facts: the narration says only what the user
  supplied or what is well known, plainly.
