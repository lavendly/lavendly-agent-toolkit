# Recipe: 3D animated feature look

Described by shape, material and light, never by a studio's name.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Family-friendly character stories, short gags, brand and channel characters,
holiday shorts. Audience: families, children with parents, general social
feeds. The ending delivers one heart moment or one held comic reaction: the
character's small want is answered on screen, and the last shot holds still
long enough to feel it.

## 2. Structure by length

Built on Veo 3.1 Fast (4, 6 or 8 s clips). A 6 s cut is generated at 8 s
and trimmed: on Veo 6 s and 8 s cost the same. Example story: Hazel the
hedgehog baker and a loaf that will not rise.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): an expressive reaction or a physical gag already
  happening, or a striking set in warm light with one small movement that
  asks a question (why is the dough flat).
- Payoff: the want answered by the hero's own action, then a held reaction
  of 1 to 1.5 s on a locked-off camera. Never end on a fade before the hold.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: a smooth virtual camera. Slow dolly, crane and truck; a snappy
  push-in only for comedy; real-lens shallow focus on close-ups. One move per
  shot, locked-off for every gag and every payoff.
- Shape language: round and soft for friends, tall and angular for
  obstacles; head large, eyes a third of the face height.
- Materials: say what each surface is: "soft subsurface skin glowing at the
  ear edges", "short groomed fur", "felt apron with visible weave",
  "glazed ceramic", "warm brushed copper".
- Light: warm key with cool fill, a rim light separating the character, soft
  bounce from the floor colour, a colour script that warms toward the payoff.
- Exact phrases: "Stylised 3D animation, rounded appealing shapes, soft
  subsurface skin and fur, warm bounce light", "global illumination, soft
  bounced fill", "shallow depth of field like a real lens", "smooth eased
  motion with anticipation and a soft settle".

## 5. Sound

- Native: Foley carries this look (squeaks, pads, puffs, clinks). Write
  each sound under SFX on Veo and keep those clips `native`.
- Narration (optional): 15 s about 30 words, 30 s about 60, 60 s about 120,
  at 2.0 to 2.3 words per second; one warm voice.
- Dialogue: one short line per clip at most, medium close-up, the speaker's
  face in frame; or none (a wordless short travels further).
- Score brief: "Whimsical chamber orchestra at about 104 BPM: pizzicato
  strings, clarinet, glockenspiel. Opens sparse on a single plucked motif,
  builds with each try, drops to one held note before the payoff, lands on
  a warm full chord as she hugs the tin. Instrumental."
- Mix: Foley-led shots `native` at 1; narrated shots `mix` at 0.3 to 0.5;
  score 0.2 to 0.3 under natural sound, 0.3 to 0.35 with `ducking: true`
  under narration.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo dialect)

Pair 1: the rising loaf (the 6 s gag, shot 3 of the 15 s cut)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Stylised 3D animation, rounded appealing shapes, soft subsurface skin and fur, warm bounce light. Hazel: a small round hedgehog standing upright like a person, chestnut spines, cream face, black button nose, round dark eyes, a flour-dusted blue apron. She stands on a stool at a floury counter beside one flat lump of dough in a tin. Medium shot, eye level, virtual 50mm, shallow focus. Warm window light from the left, cool fill. Palette: honey, cream, cornflower blue, walnut. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Stylised 3D animation, one continuous take. Smooth eased motion with anticipation and a soft settle. 0 to 2 s: Hazel leans in and pokes the flat dough once with one finger. 2 to 4 s: the dough puffs up into a round golden loaf that bulges over the tin and squashes softly as it stops. 4 to 6 s: her spines spring upright, her eyes go wide, and she holds the surprised pose. Camera: locked-off. End state: Hazel frozen in surprise beside the risen loaf. Hazel, the counter and the window light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one Hazel and one loaf, nobody else. SFX: a soft poke, a puffing whoosh, a tiny squeak. Ambient noise: a quiet warm kitchen. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the establishing shot (shot 1 of the 30 s cut; Hazel enters, so
her block rides in the motion prompt)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Stylised 3D animation, rounded appealing shapes, soft subsurface skin and fur, warm bounce light. A tiny bakery inside a hollow tree stump at dawn: a round window on the left, copper pots on hooks, a floury counter with one empty loaf tin and a wooden stool, nobody in the room. Extreme wide shot, high angle, virtual 24mm, deep focus. First warm sunlight through the round window from the left, cool blue shadows. Palette: honey, cream, cornflower blue, walnut. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Stylised 3D animation, one continuous take. Smooth eased motion. Hazel: a small round hedgehog standing upright like a person, chestnut spines, cream face, black button nose, round dark eyes, a flour-dusted blue apron. 0 to 2 s: Hazel walks in from the right edge on two legs, yawning. 2 to 4 s: she climbs onto the stool and stops beside the empty tin. Camera: slow crane down toward the counter, stopping at her eye level. End state: Hazel standing on the stool, facing the tin. The bakery and the window light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one Hazel, nobody else. SFX: soft padding footsteps, a small yawn, a stool creak. Ambient noise: early birdsong outside. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Seedance 1.5 Pro or 2.0), pair 1 rewritten in 30 to 100
words with the subject first and motion from the first frame:

```text
Hazel the hedgehog pokes the flat dough with one finger, already leaning in from the first frame; the dough puffs into a round golden loaf, bulges over the tin and squashes softly to rest; her spines spring upright and she freezes, eyes wide. Locked-off camera. Stylised 3D animation, soft subsurface fur, warm window light from the left. Exactly one hedgehog. Sound: a soft poke, a puffing whoosh, a tiny squeak. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Proportions drift between shots (head shrinks, eyes move) | the hero block verbatim in every still; head close-up as reference 1; proportion words in the bible |
| Slides toward a real animal (realistic fur, small eyes) | "stylised" in the style line, eye size and head size in the hero block, "rounded appealing shapes" |
| Plastic toy look | name the materials: subsurface skin, groomed fur, fabric weave |
| Uncanny eyes | pupils, eyelids and catchlights named; "blinks once, slowly"; both eyes look the same way |
| Squash and stretch melts the character | "squashes softly and springs back to its round shape"; smaller amount; one squash per clip |
| Busy background steals the shot | three props at most, named; shallow focus on close-ups |
| Upright in one shot, on all fours in the next | decide once; write "standing upright like a person" or "on all four paws" in every prompt |
| Hands holding objects warp | one grip, named contact ("both paws around the tin"), no finger action |

## 9. Policy guards

- No studio, film, director or character name in any prompt; translate the
  look into the words above.
- No existing characters, toys or mascots, and no design close to one (a
  famous robot, mouse, toy cowboy): change species, silhouette and colours.
- No real person turned into a 3D character without their consent.
