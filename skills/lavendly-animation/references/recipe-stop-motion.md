# Recipe: claymation, felt and stop-motion look

The handmade look: plasticine, needle felt, fabric puppets on miniature sets,
moving in small held steps. It stands out in feeds full of glossy generated
video, which is often why a brand or a parent asks for it.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Brand warmth pieces, kids' stories, music videos, quirky ads, channel
idents. The ending is a small satisfied gesture held still on a locked-off
frame, as a real animator would hold the final pose for a second or two.

Built on Wan 2.7 (whole lengths 2 to 10 s; up to 5 s is one block). Short
held shots suit the craft. Example: Dunk, a clay coffee mug, wants the sugar

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): a tactile close-up that proves the material (a
  thumbprint in clay, a felt fibre catching the light) or a quirky held-step
  movement on frame 1.
- Payoff: a small gesture and a held final pose of 1 to 2 s, the camera
  locked, the set completely still.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: mostly locked-off tabletop frames; a slow slider move at most once
  per scene; tilt-shift shallow focus that says "miniature". No handheld, no
  orbit, no crane: a real stop-motion rig rarely moves that way.
- A four-legged hero's block always includes "on all four paws" (or legs),
  even where it sits; "mouth closed" goes in every prompt where the face is
  in frame and nobody speaks.
- Scale cues: name objects at miniature scale ("a thumb-sized spoon", "a
  biscuit as big as Dunk", "fabric weave visible on the tablecloth").
- Materials: "matte plasticine with a soft sheen, thumbprints and tool
  marks"; "needle-felted wool, loose fibres at the edges, visible
  stitching"; "fabric puppets with glass-bead eyes and a faint seam across
  the face where the mouth piece joins".
- Effects made of things: smoke and clouds of cotton wool, water of
  cellophane or clear resin, rain of glass beads on threads, fire of painted
  paper flames.
- Light: small practical lamps in the set, a warm key, small hard shadows,
  a slight exposure flicker between frames.
- Motion: "stop-motion at 12 frames per second, crisp frames with no motion
  blur, slight frame-to-frame jitter"; clay surfaces shift a little every
  frame (the boil); poses hold.
- Exact phrases: "Plasticine stop-motion animation, visible fingerprints and
  tool marks, miniature handmade set", "Needle-felted stop-motion animation,
  visible wool fibres and stitching, miniature fabric set", "macro lens on a
  miniature set, shallow focus", "the clay keeps its fingerprints and the set
  does not move".

## 5. Sound

- Native: tiny Foley in proportion to the scale: squelch, pop, clink of a
  thumb-sized spoon, wool rustle, a small creak. Gag clips `native` at 1.
- Narration: a warm storyteller suits it: 15 s up to 30 words, 30 s up to
  60, 60 s up to 120, at 2.0 to 2.3 words per second.
- Dialogue: small voices, one line per clip, or none.
- Score brief: "Ukulele, accordion and toy piano at about 110 BPM, light and
  handmade; it starts with a single plucked figure, adds instruments with
  each try, drops out for the beat before the plop, then a short warm tag."
- Mix: Foley shots `native`; narrated shots `mix` at 0.4; score 0.3 with
  `ducking: true` under narration, 0.25 under natural sound.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Wan dialect)

Pair 1: Dunk wakes (the 6 s gag)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Plasticine stop-motion animation, visible fingerprints and tool marks, miniature handmade set. Dunk: a small clay coffee mug character, cream colour, a chipped rim on the left, thin clay stick arms, two big googly eyes, a wide clay smile. Dunk sits asleep, eyes shut, on a wooden kitchen table beside a thumb-sized spoon. Medium shot, table height, macro lens, shallow focus. Warm lamp light from the right, small hard shadows. Palette: cream, terracotta, butter yellow, teal. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Plasticine stop-motion animation at 12 frames per second, crisp frames with no motion blur, slight frame-to-frame jitter. 0 to 2 s: Dunk wakes, one googly eye opens, then the other. 2 to 4 s: he yawns and stretches both stick arms up; three small wisps of clay steam puff from his open top. 4 to 6 s: he holds the stretch. Camera: locked-off. End state: Dunk awake, arms up. The clay keeps its fingerprints and the set does not move. Exactly one mug character and one spoon, nobody else. Claymation. Sound: a squeaky clay stretch, a soft puff, a tiny yawn. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: Tuft in the rain (felt)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Needle-felted stop-motion animation, visible wool fibres and stitching, miniature fabric set. Tuft: a plump grey felted rabbit who hops on all four paws, long ears with pink felt insides, a stitched black nose, two black bead eyes, a small yellow felt bow on the right ear. Tuft sits on a felt hill under cotton-wool clouds, a big green felt leaf lying beside him. Medium shot, eye level, macro lens, shallow focus. Soft grey daylight from above. Palette: grey, moss green, buttercup yellow, cloud white. No text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Needle-felted stop-motion animation at 12 frames per second, crisp frames with no motion blur. 0 to 2 s: clear glass-bead raindrops start to slide down thin threads from the cotton clouds. 2 to 5 s: Tuft lifts the felt leaf over his head with both front paws and his ears fold under it. 5 to 6 s: he holds still under the leaf, mouth closed, nose twitching once. Camera: locked-off. End state: Tuft sheltered under the leaf, beads sliding past. The wool fibres and the set stay as in the opening frame; only the rabbit, the leaf and the beads move. Exactly one rabbit, nobody else. Felt style. Sound: soft taps of beads, a wool rustle. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Seedance), pair 1 in 30 to 100 words: "Dunk the clay mug
is already blinking awake from the first frame; one googly eye opens, then
the other; he yawns, stretches his clay stick arms up and three wisps of
clay steam puff out, then he holds the pose. Locked-off macro on a miniature
table set, warm lamp from the right. Plasticine stop-motion, 12 frames per
second, crisp frames, visible fingerprints. Exactly one mug. Sound: squeaky
stretch, soft puff. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Motion turns smooth and CG-like | the motion-feel line second in the prompt; "crisp frames with no motion blur" |
| Textures too clean, plastic | "thumbprints and tool marks", "loose wool fibres" in both prompts |
| Clay morphs into new shapes | small actions, one shape change per clip at most; the end shape named |
| The set reads full size | scale cues ("thumb-sized", "a biscuit as big as Dunk"), tilt-shift focus |
| Effects become realistic water or fire | name the material: cellophane water, cotton-wool smoke, paper flames |
| An animator's hand or rig appears | "only the puppets move; the set and the frame edges stay empty" |
| Fur and felt boil too much | lower motion amount; "fibres stay still except where the leaf touches them" |
| Googly eyes drift apart | "both googly eyes look the same way, the same size" |

## 9. Policy guards

- No studio names, no existing claymation or puppet characters, and no
  design close to one (a man and his dog in a knitted tank top; a gothic
  puppet girl with button eyes): change species, outfit and silhouette.
- Describe "the look of a famous studio" by its materials and light
  ([prompt-anchors.md](prompt-anchors.md) section 5).
