# Recipe: logo-free brand idents

A 4 to 8 s ident, bumper or end slate that says "this brand" without the
model ever drawing the logo. Models redraw marks, and a logo sent as a
reference comes back altered; so the ident is built from what models can
hold (colour, a simple shape, a material, a motion signature, a sound) and
ends on a clean field where the user places the real logo.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Channel intros and outros, brand bumpers between segments, end slates for
ads, event idents, app launch stings. The ending is the clean field: the
motion resolves, a large area in the brand colour stays empty and still
for 2 s or more, and the user adds the real logo there in the app's editor
(where it offers one) or in their own editor after export.

## 2. The brand toolkit (write it before any prompt)

| Element | Decide | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | two brand colours in words, plus a neutral; hex kept for the human | cobalt blue, warm white, graphite |
| Shape | one primitive that echoes the mark, never the mark | a rounded square, a ring, a chevron, a dot |
| Material | one, the same in every ident | brushed aluminium, frosted glass, matte paper, soft clay |
| Motion personality | how things move | playful: overshoot and bounce, 4-beat phrases; premium: slow ease-in-out, 8 to 16 beats; tech: precise linear move then a soft stop |
| Sound signature | a 3 to 5 note motif or one designed sound | a rising three-note marimba figure |
| The end field | where the logo will sit and how big | the right two thirds, warm white |

The cover-the-logo test: with the logo and colours swapped, would this be
the same ident for a competitor? If yes, push the shape, material or motion
until it is not.

## 3. Formats

| Format | What happens | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Assembly | 3 to 5 pieces slide or drop together into the shape | parts multiply above five |
| Light sweep | a band of light travels across the shape and the field | low |
| Drop and settle | the shape lands with an overshoot and settles | low |
| Unfold | paper or fabric unfolds into the shape | folds tangle; keep it to two folds |
| Environment | the shape as architecture or a landscape feature, camera arrives | geometry drift; keep the move short |
| Material change | one material turns into another | reads as an error unless start and end are stated |

## 5. Hook and payoff

- Hook (frame 1): the material or the shape already moving, with the first
  note of the sound motif.
- Payoff: the settle (the overshoot comes back, the light band passes), the
  last note of the motif, and the clean field held still.

## 6. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: locked-off for the ident itself; a short eased pull-back at most.
  Idents repeat thousands of times, so the composition must be exact.
- Light: a clean studio rig for the material (soft overhead plus strip
  lights for metal; soft dome light for clay); a light sweep is a moving
  band of light, not a flare.
- Colour: the two brand colours and the neutral; the end field in one of
  them, flat and even.
- Exact phrases: "four tiles slide together one after another, overshoot
  slightly and settle", "a thin band of light sweeps across from left to
  right", "the right two thirds stay an empty warm white field with nothing
  in it", "the composition holds perfectly still".

## 7. Sound

- The sonic logo carries half the ident. Attach one music track with
  `mood` and `duration` equal to the ident: "A rising three-note marimba
  figure with a soft felt piano under it, landing on a resolved major chord
  at 4 s, then a short natural decay into silence. Instrumental." 5 s
  minimum per track (1 credit).
- Native: one designed sound for the landing (a soft magnetic click), clip
  `mix` at 0.5 under the motif.
- Narration: none, or the brand name spoken once by the user's chosen voice.

## 9. Prompt pairs (Veo dialect)

Pair 1: the assembly ident (6 s)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Clean CGI product render, physically based materials, soft studio lighting. Four cobalt-blue anodised aluminium tiles, each a quarter of a rounded square, hang apart at the left third of the frame with even gaps, over a seamless warm white cyclorama with no horizon line; the right two thirds are an empty warm white field with nothing in it. Medium wide, eye level, 50mm. Soft overhead light, two strip highlights. Palette: cobalt, warm white, graphite. No text, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Medium wide, eye level, locked-off camera. 0 to 2 s: the four tiles slide together one after another, overshoot slightly and settle into one rounded square at the left third. 2 to 4 s: a thin band of light sweeps across the square and the field from left to right. 4 to 6 s: the composition holds perfectly still. End state: one cobalt rounded square at the left third, the right two thirds an empty warm white field. The right two thirds stay empty. Exactly four tiles forming one square. SFX: four soft magnetic clicks, then a faint shimmer. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the ring (an alternative ident with a light sweep)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Clean CGI product render, physically based materials, soft studio lighting. One thick brushed aluminium ring stands upright at the centre of the frame on a smooth gradient from graphite at the top to black at the floor; the space inside the ring is plain dark background with nothing in it. Medium shot, eye level, 85mm. One soft key from above, a narrow rim light from behind left. Palette: aluminium, graphite, black, one cobalt edge glow. No text, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Medium shot, eye level, locked-off camera. 0 to 3 s: a narrow band of cobalt light sweeps slowly across the ring from left to right, running along its brushed surface. 3 to 4 s: a faint cobalt glow settles on the inner edge. 4 to 6 s: the ring and the light hold perfectly still. End state: the ring at centre with a cobalt inner edge, the space inside it plain and empty. Exactly one ring. SFX: a soft rising shimmer. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Seedance 1.5 Pro), pair 1 in 30 to 100 words: "Four cobalt
aluminium tiles are already sliding together from the first frame at the
left third, overshoot slightly and settle into one rounded square; a thin
light band sweeps across from left to right; then everything holds still.
Locked-off, warm white cyclorama, the right two thirds an empty field.
Exactly four tiles. Sound: soft magnetic clicks. Clean frame: no text, no
on-screen graphics."

## 10. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The model draws a logo or letters in the field | "an empty field with nothing in it"; never write "logo" as content; re-roll |
| The shape comes out as someone else's mark | change the primitive and the material; run the cover-the-logo test |
| Tiles multiply or overlap | four or fewer pieces, the order stated, "exactly four tiles" |
| The overshoot turns to wobble | "overshoot slightly once and settle" |
| The light sweep becomes a lens flare | "a band of light running along the surface"; no flare words |
| The end field is not flat | "flat, even warm white field"; `edit_clip` `color` to nudge brightness |
| The motif does not resolve in time | music `duration` equal to the ident; the brief names the landing second |

## 11. Policy guards

- Never send any logo to a model, the user's or anyone's; the user places
  the real logo after export.
- No imitation of another brand's ident, mark, colours or sound motif.
- Trademarked shapes and characters stay out of the prompt.
