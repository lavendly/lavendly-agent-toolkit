# Recipe: title-sequence moods without rendered text

Opening sequences, channel intros and credit sequences where the pictures
set the mood and leave designed empty space; the words are added later.
Lavendly renders no on-screen text from the MCP and video models garble
letters, so the generated plates never contain the title.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Openers for films, series, podcasts shown as video, events and channels;
end-credit backgrounds. The ending is the title plate: the strongest
composition, motion slowing to a hold of 3 s or more, a large clean area
where the user places the title in the app's editor (where it offers one)
or in their own editor after export.

## 2. Moods that work

| Mood | Fits | What the plates show | Look words |
|---|---|---|---|
| Macro materials | thriller, drama, craft | ink in water, smoke curls, fabric weave, rust, sand | "macro, razor-thin focus, one hard light" |
| Evidence objects | mystery, documentary, true story | props in close-up on dark linen: a compass, keys, a pocket watch with a blank dial | "insert shots, rack focus, low key" |
| Light and refraction | tech, sci-fi, launches | prisms, light through glass, caustics on black | "clear glass and refracted light, dark studio" |
| Landscape flyover | nature, travel, documentary | mist over ridges, a river from above, dunes | "aerial, slow glide, soft dawn light" |
| Silhouettes | drama, sport, music | figures against a bright sky, no faces | "backlit silhouette, a bright sky" |
| Paper and colour fields | comedy, indie, kids | cut-paper shapes sliding over flat colour | "cut-paper shapes on flat colour" |
| Night bokeh | noir, urban, music | out-of-focus city lights, rain on glass | "soft bokeh, rain on glass, sodium and teal" |

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 1 s): one strong texture or object in motion with a sound or
  music hit on frame 1.
- Payoff: the title plate, the motion slowing into a hold, the empty area
  large and clean; the music lands on its last chord.

## 5. Camera, light, colour, layout

- Camera: slow push-ins on macro, slow slider moves for parallax, rack
  focus between two objects, top-down; the title plate locked-off or easing
  to a stop. One move per plate.
- Layout: describe the empty area as a thing ("the left third is a plain
  dark linen field with nothing on it"). Never write "title", "text" or
  "logo" as content; it invites letters. Keep the empty area in the same
  place across plates. For 9:16, keep it in the middle band, clear of the
  top 14 percent and the bottom 35 percent where the platform draws its
  interface.
- Widescreen look: compose 16:9 with the action in a central band; letterbox
  bars, if wanted, are added by the user in their editor.
- Light: low key with one hard source for mystery; soft edge light for
  tech; dawn for landscapes. One light logic for the whole sequence.
- Colour: one dominant hue for the sequence (brass and black; ink blue;
  sodium orange and teal).
- Exact phrases: "the right half stays an empty dark field with nothing in
  it", "a blank dial", "rack focus from the key to the compass", "eases to a
  stop and holds".

## 6. Sound

- A music edit: clips `off`, or `mix` at 0.3 when their texture sound
  (a tick, a drip) is part of the design; score 0.9 to 1.
- Score brief: "Low strings and a slow piano ostinato at 120 BPM in a minor
  key; a soft low hit every two bars on each plate change; a swell into the
  last plate; one held chord under the title." Picture changes are trimmed
  onto the bar lines (every 4 s at 120 BPM) with `edit_clip`.
- Narration: none; an opener with a voice is an explainer.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Veo dialect)

Pair 1: the ink plate (6 s)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Macro film of materials, razor-thin focus, tactile surfaces. A clear tank of still water against black, one thin thread of deep blue ink just entering the water at the left of the frame; the right half of the frame is an empty black field with nothing in it. Close-up, side view, 100mm macro. One hard light from above left catching the ink's edge. Palette: ink blue, black, a cold white edge. No text, no letters, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Close-up, side view, 100mm macro. 0 to 4 s: the ink blooms downward in slow curling clouds on the left side, folding over itself. 4 to 6 s: the bloom slows and hangs still. Camera: slow push-in of a few centimetres, easing to a stop. End state: a still blue bloom on the left, the right half an empty black field. The right half stays empty. Exactly one ink bloom. Macro film of materials. SFX: a soft low water hush. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the compass (the title plate of the 15 s opener)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Macro film of materials, razor-thin focus, tactile surfaces. An old brass compass with a blank dial lies on dark grey linen at the right of the frame, its needle still; a brass key lies blurred in the foreground; the left third is a plain dark linen field with nothing on it. Medium close-up, 45 degrees above, 85mm. One low hard light from the right, deep shadows. Palette: brass, charcoal, oxblood. No text, no letters, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Medium close-up, 45 degrees above. 0 to 3 s: rack focus from the blurred key in the foreground to the compass; the compass needle swings slowly. 3 to 7 s: the needle settles and the frame holds still. Camera: locked-off. End state: the compass sharp at the right, the left third an empty dark linen field. The left third stays empty. Exactly one compass and one key. Macro film of materials. SFX: a faint metallic tick as the needle settles. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Runway, motion only): pair 1 as "Ink blooms downward in
slow curling clouds on the left and hangs still; the camera pushes in a few
centimetres and stops; the right half stays dark and empty."

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Letters or pseudo-text appear in the empty area | describe the area as a plain surface; never say "title"; re-roll that plate |
| The empty area fills with objects | "nothing in it" and a lock sentence for the area; fewer props |
| Dials, letters and maps grow writing | blank dials, blank envelopes; no maps or books with visible pages |
| Plates feel unrelated | one light logic, one hue, one material family, the empty area in the same place |
| Cuts fall between beats | trims at multiples of the bar length (4 s at 120 BPM) |
| The hold drifts or pushes in | "locked-off camera on a tripod, the frame does not move" |

## 10. Policy guards

- No imitation of a known film's or series' title sequence; describe the
  mood, never the work.
- No real people's faces in silhouette plates presented as specific people.
- No flashing faster than three times per second.
