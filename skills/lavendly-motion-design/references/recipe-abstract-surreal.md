# Recipe: abstract and surreal art films

Images that break physics calmly: a door standing in the sea, furniture
hanging over a desert, a room filling with clouds. Generative video is
strong here, as long as each shot holds one impossible idea in a clean
composition and every transformation is written with its start and end.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

Art pieces, gallery loops, brand mood films, music visuals, poetry films,
channel openers. Audience: viewers who watch for images and feeling. The
ending resolves the motif: the impossible thing completes its change and
the frame holds in stillness, or the first image returns, changed.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook (frame 1): the impossible image is already there, calm and exactly
  composed, with one thing moving; nothing explains it.
- Payoff: the transformation completes on screen (the door opens, the room
  fills) and the frame holds still long enough to look at.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: static tableaux and very slow push-ins; symmetry; slow motion for
  falling and floating things (under 4 s). No handheld, no orbit around an
  impossible object (it reveals the trick and drifts).
- One surreal element per shot. Clean grounds: sea, desert, white rooms,
  empty squares with long shadows.
- Transformations: write the start state, the change, the end state
  ("the room is empty; low cloud rises from the floor; at the end the cloud
  reaches the window sills").
- Match cuts instead of dissolves: the end composition of one shot and the
  opening still of the next share shape, position and size.
- Light: one clear source per film: hard noon sun with crisp short shadows,
  low dawn light with long shadows, flat overcast for dream calm.
- Colour: describe painting moods in plain words, never a painter's name:
  "muted ochre and slate blue, long shadows, an empty plaza with arcades",
  "pale pink dawn and sea green", "chalk white with one red object".
- Exact phrases: "Surreal still-life film, calm and exact, long shadows,
  muted ochre and slate palette", "perfectly still reflections",
  "symmetrical composition", "the only object in the landscape".

## 5. Sound

- Native: one exaggerated single sound per shot (a door latch, sand hiss,
  a lantern creak), with an ambient bed: `native` at 0.8.
- Narration: optional, poetic, sparse: 1.5 words per second (15 s up to
  20 words, 30 s up to 45, 60 s up to 90), long pauses between lines.
- Score brief: "A low drone in D with sparse felt piano notes at about 60
  BPM; one reversed piano swell into the final shot; the drone thins to
  silence on the held last image." For three movements, name the change per
  movement (drone, then a pulse, then piano alone).
- Mix: score 0.4 to 0.5 with `ducking: true` under narration; without
  narration, clips `mix` at 0.6 and the score at 0.6.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo dialect)

Pair 1: the door in the sea (6 s)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Surreal still-life film, calm and exact, long shadows. A single green wooden door in its frame stands upright in calm shallow sea water at dawn, no walls, the door closed, a pale pink sky, perfectly still reflections, the only object in the sea. Wide shot, eye level, 35mm, deep focus, symmetrical composition. Low dawn light from the left. Palette: sea green, pale pink, pearl grey. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Wide shot, eye level, locked-off camera. 0 to 2 s: the sea is still; the green door stands closed. 2 to 5 s: the door swings slowly open by itself, inward, away from camera. 5 to 6 s: through the open doorway a sunny summer meadow fills the frame of the door, while the sea around it stays grey and calm. End state: the door wide open on the meadow. The sea, the sky and the light around the door stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one door. Surreal still-life film, calm and exact. SFX: a soft latch click, a creak. Ambient noise: lapping water, then birdsong through the door. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: rising sand (shot 3 of the 30 s poem)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Surreal still-life film, calm and exact, long shadows. An armchair, a brass floor lamp and a full wooden bookshelf hang motionless in the air above an empty desert, each about a metre above its own shadow, nothing else in the landscape. Medium shot, low angle, 50mm. Hard white noon sun from above, crisp short shadows on pale sand. Palette: ochre, slate blue, bone white. No text, no letters.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Medium shot, low angle. 0 to 3 s: the three objects hang still; a thin stream of sand begins to trickle upward from the top of the bookshelf into the sky. 3 to 8 s: the stream rises steadily and thins out high above the frame, while the armchair turns slowly a few degrees in place. Camera: very slow push-in toward the bookshelf, stopping at a medium close-up. End state: the stream still rising, the objects in place. Exactly three objects in the air. The desert and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Surreal still-life film. SFX: a fine sand hiss. Ambient noise: a low desert wind. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Wan 2.7): subject, scene, motion, then aesthetic words: "A
green wooden door stands alone in calm shallow sea at dawn. It swings open
by itself onto a sunny meadow; the sea stays grey and still. Locked-off
camera. Low dawn light, symmetrical composition, low contrast, pale pink and
sea green. Surreal. Sound: a latch, lapping water, birdsong. Clean frame: no
text, no on-screen graphics."

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| "AI dream" clutter, many strange things | one impossible element per shot; "the only object in the landscape" |
| A transformation reads as an error | start state, change and end state written; a locked camera |
| Faces appear in clouds, walls or sand | name the material and its form ("smooth low cloud, a flat top"); no human words near textures |
| Signs or book spines grow letters | blank spines, "no text, no letters" |
| Morph never completes | one change per clip with its end state; shorter clip |
| Every shot drifts in colour | one palette line verbatim; one light per film |
| A cut meant as a match looks random | the shape, its position and size written identically in the end state of one shot and the still of the next |

## 9. Policy guards

- No painter, photographer, director or film named; describe the palette,
  light and composition.
- No real people; no real event or place shown as if documented.
- No flashing faster than three times per second.
