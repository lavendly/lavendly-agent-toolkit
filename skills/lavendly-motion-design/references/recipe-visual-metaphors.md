# Recipe: data and product visual metaphors

A number, a process or a benefit shown as a physical thing: savings as
coins filling a jar, simplicity as tangled yarn becoming a neat ball, speed
as a paper boat outrunning a stone. Generated video cannot draw a precise
chart, so the metaphor carries the feeling and the exact number lives in the
narration and its captions.

## 1. The honest rule for numbers

- Video models invent bars, axes, counts and labels. Never generate a chart,
  a graph, a percentage or a dashboard.
- The exact figure is spoken (spelled out: "forty percent") and shown by the
  captions burned from the voice track; open the render to check how the
  caption writes it.
- The picture shows a proportion physically and approximately ("the jar is
  about three quarters full"); when the proportion is illustrative, the
  narration says so ("roughly", "for example").
- Never invent a number. With no source, the film uses no number.
- A real chart the user made elsewhere is placed by the user in the app;
  never ask a model to animate it (labels and bars will change).

## 2. Metaphor library

| Idea | Metaphor | Count and proportion |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | a seedling unfurling; a stack of wooden blocks rising | one plant; five blocks or fewer |
| Savings, accumulation | coins dropping into a glass jar | the jar from empty to about three quarters |
| Share of a whole | two identical glasses filled to different levels | two glasses, levels named in words |
| Speed | a paper boat gliding past a sinking stone; a lit fuse | two objects |
| Security | a vault door closing; layers of glass sliding into place | three layers |
| Simplicity | tangled red yarn untangling into a neat ball | one strand |
| Connection | threads stretching between pins on a board | five pins or fewer |
| Time saved | sand falling through an hourglass that is flipped early | one hourglass |
| Scale, big numbers | one grain of sand, then a dune | no count, a mass |
| Process, workflow | a marble rolling down a wooden track through three gates | one marble, three gates |
| Before and after | the same object from the same angle, changed | matched framing, hard cut |
| Risk reduced | a wobbling tower of blocks steadied by one block | five blocks |

Rules: one metaphor per shot; five countable objects or fewer, or a mass with
no count; comparisons side by side in identical containers; the end state
named ("filled to just below the shoulder of the jar").

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook (0 to 2 s): the problem as an object already misbehaving (the knot,
  the leaking jar), with the first line of narration naming it.
- Payoff: the resolved object held still while the narration says the one
  number or the one benefit.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: locked-off and slow push-ins; the metaphor must read at a glance.
  Top-down for counts and levels; eye level for comparisons.
- Light: bright, clean and even (high key) for business and education; one
  soft key with clean shadows; nothing dark and moody unless the brief
  wants it.
- Set: one neutral table or paper sweep for the whole film, so the objects
  feel like one argument.
- Colour: one accent colour for the "after" state (the red yarn, the gold
  coins) against neutral greys and whites.
- Exact phrases: "filled to about three quarters", "exactly five wooden
  blocks", "two identical glasses side by side", "the knot loosens and the
  strand slides free", "comes to rest and holds still".

## 6. Sound and narration

- Narration-led: 2.3 to 2.5 words per second; 15 s about 35 words, 30 s
  about 70, 60 s about 140. One idea per sentence, the number spoken in
  words, one voice.
- Native: small object sounds (coin clinks, sand hiss, yarn rustle) `mix`
  at 0.3.
- Score brief: "Light plucked strings and marimba at about 100 BPM, curious
  and warm, building a little with each point, resolving on the payoff."
  0.3 with `ducking: true`.
- Captions on (`youtube` or `caption` for a business audience).

## 8. Prompt pairs (Veo dialect)

Pair 1: the yarn (6 s)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Clean CGI product render, physically based materials, soft studio lighting. One tangled knot of thick red wool yarn lies on a pale grey table, one loose end trailing to the right, nothing else on the table; plain light grey wall behind. Close-up, eye level, 85mm, shallow focus. Soft even key light from above left, clean soft shadows. Palette: wool red, pale grey, warm white. No text, no letters, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Close-up, eye level, locked-off camera. 0 to 3 s: the knot loosens by itself and the strand slides free, loop by loop. 3 to 6 s: the strand winds itself into one neat round ball that rolls a little to the left and comes to rest. End state: one neat red ball of yarn on the table, holding still. The table, the wall and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one strand of yarn. SFX: a soft wool rustle, a gentle stop. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Pair 2: the jar (shot 3 of the 30 s cut)

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Clean CGI product render, physically based materials, soft studio lighting. One empty clear glass jar with a wide mouth stands on a pale grey table, a few gold coins already in the air above its mouth, nothing else on the table; plain light grey wall behind. Medium shot, eye level, 50mm. Soft even light from above left, clear glass edge highlights. Palette: gold, clear glass, pale grey, warm white. No text, no letters, no logo.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Medium shot, eye level. 0 to 6 s: gold coins fall steadily into the jar and pile up until the jar is filled to about three quarters, then the last coin lands and the pile settles. Camera: slow truck right, a few centimetres, easing to a stop. End state: the jar about three quarters full of gold coins. Exactly one jar. The table and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. SFX: bright coin clinks, slowing to one last clink. No music. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family (Wan 2.7), pair 1: "A tangled knot of thick red wool yarn on a
pale grey table. The knot loosens and the strand winds itself into one neat
ball that comes to rest. Locked-off close-up. Soft even light, high key, low
contrast, red accent on grey. Sound: wool rustle. Clean frame: no text, no
on-screen graphics."

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| A chart, numbers or labels appear | never ask for them; "no text, no letters"; numbers in the narration |
| Counts wrong (six blocks instead of five) | five or fewer, "exactly five"; or a mass with no count |
| Comparison containers differ in size | "two identical glasses side by side" in both prompts |
| Coins or grains multiply in the air | name the fill state at the end; one pour path |
| The metaphor is unclear | one metaphor per shot; the narration names it in the first line |
| Physics looks wrong (yarn melts) | slower, one action, the end state named |

## 10. Policy guards

- No invented statistics, no "results" without a source, no health or
  financial outcome shown as proof.
- Illustrative data is said to be illustrative in the narration.
- No real company's logo, product or chart in the frame.
