# Recipe: science fiction

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: one idea about the future, technology or the unknown, made
  concrete in a world the viewer believes for a minute. Wonder or dread.
- Audience: genre fans, concept-art audiences, worldbuilders, pitch decks.
- The ending shows the consequence of the idea on a person: the scale
  revealed, the machine's choice, the door opened. A world without a person
  in it is a mood reel, not a story.

## 2. The worldbuilding rules

- One idea per film (the sky has a door; the robot learns to lie). Every
  shot serves it.
- Scale needs a person: a figure in the lower third of an extreme wide.
- Technology is shown working, not explained: a light that answers, a
  machine that moves with weight.
- Screens, HUDs, readouts and signs come out as gibberish: keep screens
  glowing blank, abstract light patterns, or turned away. "The display
  shows only soft moving blue light, no symbols."
- A world palette and a material list written once and pasted into every
  still ("brushed aluminium, salt-white ground, cobalt light").
- Sub-styles without names: near-future realism (familiar streets, one new
  object), retro-futurism (rounded 1960s forms, chrome and pastel),
  neon-noir city (rain, magenta and cyan signs with no letters), used
  future (scratched paint, taped cables, dust), space interior (cold strip
  lights, floating objects), alien landscape (one impossible feature in a
  real geology). Never a franchise or film title.

## 3. Structures by length

Model lengths for Wan 2.7 or FLUX 3 Video (5 s for up to 5 s, 10 s for up
to 10 s; FLUX makes 5 s minimum). On Veo use 4 or 8 s.

### 30 s: discovery

| # | Size, lens | Move | Action | Beat | Cut | Generate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Close-up, 85mm | locked-off | A repair robot's single round lens refocuses on camera | Hook | 3 s | 5 s |
| 2 | Wide, 24mm | slow pan right | A cluttered repair bay, cables, one work lamp | World | 5 s | 5 s |

### 60 s: three acts

| Act | Time | Shots |
|---|---|---|
| World and want | 0 to 12 s | Hook close-up, establishing extreme wide with a figure, a routine action |
| Inciting idea | 12 to 20 s | The anomaly appears (insert plus reaction) |
| Attempts | 20 to 42 s | Approach, a failed contact, a second attempt that costs something; 5 shots |
| Climax | 42 to 52 s | The contact or the choice, static, held |
| Consequence | 52 to 60 s | The scale revealed: a pull-back or crane that shows what changed |

About 12 shots.

### 90 s: three acts with a midpoint reveal

The 60 s structure with a 30 s middle that reveals the rule of the world
(the ring is a gate, the robot is the last one): an establishing shot of the
new information, two reaction shots, a quiet shot of the person alone with
it. About 17 shots.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: one impossible thing presented calmly in frame 1 (an object in the
  sky, a lens refocusing, a shadow with no source).
- Payoff: the consequence on a person, then scale: a static held shot or a
  slow pull-back that reveals more of the world than we knew.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: slow crane and pull-back for scale; locked-off for machines
  working (precision reads still); slow push-in for contact; a wide lens
  low to the ground for alien landscapes.
- Light: one motivated source per scene (cold overhead strips, a warning
  lamp, a planet's low sun, the device's own glow). Volumetric beams only
  where there is dust or haze in the world.
- Colour: one world palette with one accent: cobalt and salt-white;
  amber and steel; magenta and cyan on black.

Exact phrases that work:

- "Science fiction film still, large-format camera look"
- "the figure small in the lower third of frame, the structure filling the sky"
- "cold white overhead strip lights, a red warning lamp pulsing on the far wall"
- "the display shows only soft moving blue light, no symbols"
- "brushed aluminium with fine scratches, a strip of grey tape on the left arm"
- "moves slowly and precisely, each joint stopping before the next moves"

Robots and creatures get a locked description like any character: body
shape, material, colour, size cue, one asymmetric mark, how they move.

## 6. Sound

- Native: machine hums, servo whirs, wind on a plain, ship ambience;
  `native` on shots whose sound is the point, `mix` at 0.3 under narration.
- Space exteriors: choose silence or score only, and keep it consistent.
- Score: "Slow analogue synth pad and a low pulse at about 60 BPM; a single
  high bell tone when the anomaly appears; swells under the climax; ends on
  an unresolved chord. Instrumental." Volume 0.35, `ducking` on under voice.
- Narration (a log, a transmission): 2.3 words per second, past tense or
  present, plain; captions on.

## 8. Prompt pairs

### Pair 1: the ring (FLUX 3 Video, 15 s shot 1)

Still frame:

```text
Science fiction film still, large-format camera look. Extreme wide shot, low angle, 24mm lens. Petra: a woman in her thirties in a dusty white field jacket and dark goggles pushed up on her forehead, standing small in the lower third at the edge of a white salt flat. A vast matte-black ring hangs motionless in the pale sky above the horizon. Low morning sun from the left, long shadows on the salt. Palette: salt white, cobalt, black.
```

Motion:

```text
Science fiction film shot, large-format camera look. Slow crane up and back over the whole shot, ending high above Petra. 0 to 3 s: Petra stands still; heat shimmer moves over the salt. 3 to 5 s: the ring turns a quarter turn on its axis, slowly and silently, and stops. Petra, the salt flat and the sun stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one person in frame, nobody else. Sound: wind over the flat, a very low hum that rises as the ring turns. Clean frame: no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

### Pair 2: the repair bay (Wan 2.7, 30 s shot 3, with a style plate)

Still frame:

```text
Science fiction film still, large-format camera look. Medium shot, eye level, 50mm lens. Unit 7: a slim repair robot, brushed aluminium body with fine scratches, one round amber lens for a face, a strip of grey tape on its left forearm. It sits at a cluttered workbench, holding a small brass music box. One warm work lamp from the left, the bay behind in cool shadow, cables on the wall. Palette: amber, steel grey, deep blue.
```

Motion:

```text
Unit 7 solders a wire inside the brass music box with slow, precise movements, each joint stopping before the next moves; a thin curl of solder smoke rises into the lamp light. Static camera, eye level. Reference 1 is the style plate for palette and light only; ignore its content. Unit 7, the bench and the lamp light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one robot in frame, nobody else. Light source: practical lamp, side light, low contrast, medium shot, warm and cool. Sound: a soft solder hiss, servo whir. Clean frame: no text, no symbols on any surface.
```

### Pair 3: contact (Veo 3.1 Fast)

Still frame:

```text
Science fiction film still, large-format camera look. Medium close-up, eye level, 85mm lens. Petra: a woman in her thirties, sunburnt olive skin, short black hair, a thin scar across her chin, dusty white field jacket, goggles on her forehead. She looks up and slightly right, her face lit by a cold blue glow from above. Pale sky behind her, out of focus. Palette: cobalt, salt white, warm skin.
```

Motion:

```text
Science fiction film shot. Locked-off camera. 0 to 3 s: the blue light on Petra's face pulses slowly, twice. 3 to 6 s: she does not blink; her lips part; she breathes out once. 6 to 8 s: she lowers her goggles over her eyes with one hand. Petra, her jacket and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one person in frame, nobody else. SFX: a deep tone rising and falling with the pulse. Ambient noise: wind. Clean frame: no text, no subtitles, no on-screen graphics.
```

Second family notes: on Seedance, give each reference one job ("reference 1
is Petra's face and jacket only; reference 2 is the salt flat location only;
ignore their poses and light").

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Gibberish on screens, panels and signs | Blank glowing screens, abstract light, signs turned away; "no symbols on any surface" |
| Generic "sci-fi" clutter, every surface lit | One idea, one source, one accent colour; a material list |
| The robot or creature changes shape between shots | Locked description with joints, materials and the asymmetric mark; a reference on Seedance or Wan |
| Scale reads as a miniature | A person for scale, a low wide lens, haze in the distance |
| Floating or zero-g motion melts | Slow motion of one object at a time; "drifts slowly, rotating once" |
| Machines move like rubber | "moves slowly and precisely, each joint stopping before the next moves" |
| A second ship, ring or robot appears | Count it: "exactly one ring in the sky" |

## 10. Policy guards

- No franchise ships, uniforms, robots or alien species; no film titles or
  studio names. Describe the forms and materials.
- No real space agency logos or real astronauts.
