# Recipe: period pieces

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: a story set in another time, where the era is felt in light,
  materials, wardrobe and manners, not announced. Period drama, historical
  fiction, a family story set in the 1950s, a fable in a medieval village.
- Audience: drama audiences, history lovers, heritage and museum creators
  (fiction clearly framed as fiction).
- The ending: the same as the drama it carries (a change shown and held);
  the era makes the stakes (a letter that takes weeks, a door a woman may
  not walk through).

## 2. Era control

Models drift to the present: a modern window frame, a zip, a plastic chair,
electric light in 1840. Control the era three ways in every still prompt:

1. The decade and place in the style line ("Period drama still, 1890s
   London, warm colour film look").
2. Period light: the only sources that existed (candles, oil lamps, gas
   lamps, early bulbs, daylight).
3. A positive object list: name what is in the room, all of it period, so
   the model has no empty space to fill ("only a wooden table, a pewter jug,
   a tallow candle and a wool blanket").

Anachronisms the model loves (state the replacement): electric light in
pre-electric rooms (write "lit only by candles"); zips and plastic (write
the fastening: "bone buttons", "laces"); modern haircuts (write the period
cut); sneakers (write the shoe); modern glass windows (write "small leaded
panes"); paved roads (write "packed earth", "cobbles").

## 3. Structures by length

Model lengths for Veo 3.1 Fast (4 or 8 s).

### 6 s: a period vignette

| # | Size, angle, lens | Move | Action | Cut | Generate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Close-up, 85mm | locked-off | By candlelight, a woman breaks the wax seal on a letter | 3 s | 4 s |
| 2 | Medium close-up, 85mm | locked-off | She reads; her hand goes to her mouth | 3 s | 4 s |

### 15 s: the news

| # | Size, angle, lens | Move | Action | Cut | Generate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide, 35mm | locked-off | A farmhouse kitchen in the 1890s at dusk; a boy runs in with a letter | 4 s | 4 s |
| 2 | Insert, 100mm | locked-off | The wax seal cracks under her thumb | 3 s | 4 s |
| 3 | Medium close-up, 85mm | slow push-in | She reads; her breath stops | 4 s | 4 s |
| 4 | Wide, 35mm | locked-off | She sits down slowly at the table; the boy stands still; held | 4 s | 4 s |

### 30 s: three acts in miniature

Hook (a detail of the era in motion: a gas lamp lit by a pole), world (the
street or the room, with people doing period work), want (the protagonist
and what they want, seen), inciting (a letter, a knock, a uniform at the
door), choice (a close-up held), act (the step through the door), new state
(the wide, held). Seven shots: 4, 4, 4, 3, 5, 4, 6 s.

### 60 s: a period drama scene

The 30 s structure with two complications in the middle (a rule of the era
in the way: a father's refusal, a fare she cannot pay), and one dialogue
exchange in short period lines (four to six lines, [dialogue-scenes.md](dialogue-scenes.md)).
Eleven to thirteen shots.

### 90 s: two times

Two headings, the same place decades apart (1952 and 1972), each with its
own wardrobe, light and look line. The cut between them is a dip to black
and a matched framing (the same doorway). Fifteen to eighteen shots.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: a period action in close-up that only that era has (a quill
  scratching, a gas lamp being lit, a telegram handed over), in frame 1.
- Payoff: the change, held, in the era's own light (the candle burning
  down, the train leaving).

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: classical. Locked-off and slow push-ins; dolly moves along a
  street at walking pace; the camera behaves like the era's films when the
  look asks for it (static, frontal, symmetrical for the 1920s; zooms for the
  1970s).
- Light: only the era's sources; candle and oil lamp warmth with dark
  corners; foggy daylight; one hard window.
- Colour: the era's photography in plain words (see the table).

Exact phrases that work:

- "Period drama still, 1890s farmhouse, warm colour film look"
- "lit only by two tallow candles on the table, warm and flickering, the corners dark"
- "a high-collared cream blouse with bone buttons, a dark wool skirt"
- "only period objects in the room: a wooden table, a pewter jug, a cast-iron stove"
- "packed earth road, wooden cart wheels, no paving"
- "1950s three-strip colour film look, saturated primaries, soft highlights"

## 6. Sound

- Native ambience of the era: horses on cobbles, a ticking clock, a coal
  stove, a gramophone far off, rain on a slate roof. `native` on period
  sound shots.
- Dialogue in plain period diction, short ("You will not go." "I already
  have."); avoid modern idioms.
- Score: instruments the era would know, unless the film chooses contrast.
  "Solo violin and a small string ensemble, about 60 BPM, restrained; a
  three-note figure on the letter; swells once on the choice; ends on a
  quiet unison note. Instrumental." Volume 0.3, `ducking` on under narration.
- Narration: a letter read aloud is the classic device (one voice, 2.3 words
  per second, captions in `youtube` serif).

## 8. Prompt pairs

### Pair 1: the seal (Veo 3.1 Fast, 15 s shot 2)

Still frame:

```text
Period drama still, 1890s farmhouse, warm colour film look. Insert shot, 100mm macro lens. A woman's work-worn hands, a thin brass wedding band, the cuff of a high-collared cream blouse with bone buttons, holding a folded letter closed with a dark red wax seal. Rough wooden table below. Lit only by one tallow candle to the right, warm and flickering, the background dark. Palette: cream, wax red, candle amber, dark wood.
```

Motion:

```text
Period drama, warm colour film look, one continuous take. Locked-off camera. 0 to 2 s: her thumb presses under the seal. 2 to 3 s: the wax cracks in two and the letter springs open a little. 3 to 4 s: her fingers stop, holding it. Her hands, the ring, the letter and the candle light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly two hands in frame, nobody else. Sound: a dry crack of wax, the candle flame fluttering, a clock ticking. Clean frame: no handwriting visible, no text, no on-screen graphics.
```

### Pair 2: the diner (Kling v3 Pro, 1950s)

Still frame:

```text
Period drama still, 1955 roadside diner, three-strip colour film look, saturated primaries. Medium shot, eye level, 50mm lens. June: a woman in her twenties, a red full-skirted dress with a white collar, dark hair in short pin curls, a small mole above her lip. She sits alone on a chrome stool at a long counter, a cup of coffee in front of her. Fluorescent tubes above, bright afternoon sun through the windows behind. Palette: red, chrome, mint green, cream.
```

Motion:

```text
Scene: a 1950s roadside diner, afternoon. Character: June on the chrome stool at the counter. Action: she stirs her coffee slowly, stops, and looks at the door to screen left as a bell rings above it; she does not turn her body. Camera: static, eye level. Audio: a door bell, a spoon on china, a far radio playing a soft swing tune. June, her dress and the counter stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one person in frame, nobody else. Clean frame: no text, no signs with letters.
```

### Pair 3: the lamplighter (Seedance 2.0, with references)

Still frame:

```text
Period drama still, 1890s London street at dusk, warm colour film look. Wide shot, eye level, 35mm lens. A lamplighter: a thin old man in a long dark wool coat and a bowler hat, holding a tall brass-tipped pole up to an unlit iron gas lamp. Wet cobbles, brick terraces with small-paned windows, light fog. Cool blue dusk with a faint warm glow in one window. Palette: slate blue, brass, wet black.
```

Motion:

```text
Reference 1 is the lamplighter's face and coat only; ignore its background and pose. Reference 2 is the street's look and fog only. The lamplighter, already reaching up from the first frame, touches the pole's flame to the lamp; the gas catches and the lamp glows warm; he lowers the pole and walks on to the right at an old man's pace. Static camera, eye level. The street, the fog and the dusk light stay as in the opening frame. Exactly one person in frame, nobody else. Audio: a soft gas pop, footsteps on wet cobbles, a horse far off. No music. Clean frame: no text.
```

Second family notes: on Veo, put the era first in the style line and the
light source second; Veo follows "lit only by" strictly.

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Modern objects creep in (a plastic chair, a power socket) | The positive object list; no empty surfaces left unnamed |
| Electric light in a candle-lit room | "lit only by", the source named, the corners dark |
| Costumes drift to generic "old-fashioned" | Named fabrics and fastenings; a costume reference on Seedance |
| Handwriting, signs and newspapers garble | Letters folded or seen from the back, signs turned away, "no handwriting visible" |
| Period speech sounds modern | Short plain lines in the plan, checked word by word |
| Mixed eras in one film | One era line per heading, pasted verbatim |

## 10. Policy guards

- No real historical figures by name or likeness; fictional people in a
  real era.
- Real events are a backdrop, never staged as documentary footage; no
  atrocities depicted; sensitive histories handled with care and context.
- No real regimental, national or political insignia used as decoration.
