# Recipe: jewellery and watches

Rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, watches, small precious objects. The
sale is light on metal and stone, and the piece on skin. The piece must be
exact to the prong and the stone count; the pace is slower than any other
ad; dials carry no numerals or lettering the model would invent.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: gift buyers and collectors on Instagram, Meta feed, YouTube;
  product pages; boutique screens.
- The viewer should see the sparkle move and the piece sit on a hand, wrist
  or neck at true scale.
- The ending: the piece at rest on velvet or in its open box (the
  packshot), held 3 to 4 s, the call to action spoken softly, space for the
  logo.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: sparkle in motion at frame 1: a stone turning under point lights,
  a chain sliding through fingers, a watch crown turning, a box lid opening
  on light. Silence or one soft sound.
- Payoff: the piece on skin at true scale, then at rest in its box. The
  last shot is the stillest.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 100mm macro for stones, prongs, links and crowns; 85mm for hands,
  wrists and necklines; a hidden turntable turning 45 to 90 degrees; slow
  slides along a band or bracelet; locked-off holds. Never a fast move.
- Light: "several small hard point lights from different directions
  creating moving sparkles in the facets", "a large diffusion panel above
  for clean gradients on the metal", "white bounce cards filling the
  facets", on "black velvet" or "dark slate". On skin: "soft window light"
  or "warm candlelight from the right, small and close".
- Colour: black or deep neutral backgrounds; true metal colour ("warm
  yellow gold", "cool white platinum", "rose gold"); "Palette: black, warm
  gold, white".
- Watches: "a plain cream dial with thin baton hands and no numerals, the
  hands at ten past ten and still"; the seconds hand stays out of the
  description so it does not race.

## 5. Sound

- Native: almost silent; a faint chime, a box hinge, a crown click, a chain
  settling on velvet.
- Narration: 6 s up to 10 words or none; 15 s 22 to 30 (slow, close); 30 s
  50 to 62; 60 s 110 to 130. Few words, long pauses.
- Score: "Sparse felt piano and a soft string pad around 70 BPM. Opens on a
  single high note as the stone turns, builds gently through the story, ends
  on one sustained chord under the box. Instrumental, never busy."
- Mix: sparkle and box clips `native` 1 when no narration sits over them;
  narrated clips `mix` 0.2; score 0.3 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

**Pair 1: the turning stone** (opens on the macro packshot)

Still:

```text
Premium jewellery commercial still. Macro shot, 100mm lens, very shallow focus. A yellow gold ring with a single round diamond held in four prongs rests upright on black velvet. Several small hard point lights create bright sparkles in the facets, soft gradient reflections on the band. Palette: black, warm gold, white. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera. The ring turns slowly on a hidden turntable by about sixty degrees over the whole shot, then stops. Sparkles flash across the facets as it turns; the gradients slide along the band. The ring's shape, prongs and single stone stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one ring, one stone. SFX: a faint high chime once. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: on the hand**

Still:

```text
Premium jewellery commercial still. Close-up, 85mm lens, shallow focus. A woman's left hand with short natural nails rests on dark linen, a yellow gold ring with a single round diamond on the ring finger. Soft window light from the right, a small hard point light catching the stone. Natural skin texture. Palette: warm gold, linen grey, soft skin tones. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in toward the ring over the whole shot. 1 to 3 s: the hand turns slightly toward the window light and settles; a sparkle crosses the stone. The hand, the ring and the linen stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one hand, one ring, five fingers. SFX: a soft fabric sound. Ambient noise: a quiet room. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the watch crown** (a watch)

Still:

```text
Premium watch commercial still. Macro shot, 100mm lens. A round brushed steel watch with a polished bezel and a plain cream dial with thin baton hands and no numerals, the hands at ten past ten, lying on dark slate, the crown toward camera. A large soft panel above, one small point light on the bezel. Palette: steel, cream, slate grey. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera at macro distance. 0 to 3 s: a fingertip enters from the right and turns the crown a quarter turn, then withdraws. 3 to 4 s: a highlight slides across the bezel. The case, the dial and the hands stay exactly as in the opening frame, the hands still at ten past ten. Exactly one watch, one fingertip. SFX: a fine mechanical click. No music. Clean frame, no text, no numerals.
```

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Extra stones or prongs appear | open on the packshot; "exactly one stone, four prongs"; turn under 90 degrees |
| Prongs melt during a turn | smaller turn, slower; trim before the melt |
| Dial numerals or brand text invented | "a plain dial with no numerals", no brand words |
| Watch hands race or swap | "the hands at ten past ten and still"; no seconds hand named |
| Sparkle flickers like noise | fewer point lights named ("three small point lights"); slower turn |
| A sixth finger or fused fingers | "five fingers", the hand still, one small turn |
| The ring changes size on the hand | the still states its scale; the reference wired; shorter clip |

## 9. Policy guards

- Metal, stone, carat, clarity, "natural" or "lab-grown" and certification
  claims only as the user gives them.
- The piece shown is the piece sold: stone count, setting and colour as in
  the packshot.
- No other houses' designs, monograms or signature motifs in prompts.
- Proposal and gift stories cast adults; no celebrity likeness.
