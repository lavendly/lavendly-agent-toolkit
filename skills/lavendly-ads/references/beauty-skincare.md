# Recipe: beauty and skincare

Serums, creams, cleansers, makeup, haircare, fragrance. The sale is the
feel: texture, ritual, the moment on skin. The product must be exact, the
skin must look like skin, and no result may be shown as proof.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: buyers scrolling Reels, TikTok and Meta feed; product pages;
  YouTube pre-roll for launches.
- The viewer should want to touch it: the texture, how it moves, how it
  sits on skin.
- The ending: the product beside the skin or hand it was just used on, or
  the packshot alone, held 2 to 3 s, the call to action spoken, clean space
  for the user's logo and offer card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the texture in motion at frame 1 (a falling drop, a cream peak
  folding, a lipstick bullet twisting up), with its sound. Never a face
  saying hello, never a logo.
- Payoff: the feel shown, not claimed: a sheen, a fingertip with nothing on
  it, a calm face. Then the packshot, still, held.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 100mm macro for textures, 85mm portrait for faces, top-down for
  swatches and swirls. Locked-off or slow push-ins; light moves more than
  the camera. Orbits on bottles stay under 90 degrees.
- Light for faces: "clamshell beauty light, a large soft source above the
  camera and a reflector under the chin, catchlights at twelve o'clock", or
  "soft north-facing window light from camera left".
- Light for products: "soft diffused light from above with a backlight
  making the serum glow honey-gold"; for clear glass "two tall strip lights
  at the sides drawing bright edge lines"; for jars "one large softbox
  reflected as a long gradient across the lid".
- Colour: clean neutral white balance; palettes from the product ("Palette:
  white, amber, sand" or "blush, cream, soft grey"); dewy, not glossy-wet.
- Skin: always "natural skin texture, fine pores" and one real mark (a few
  freckles, a small mole). Never "flawless", "porcelain", "airbrushed".

## 5. Sound

- Native: the drip, the cap click, the pump, a soft skin sound. Keep the
  hook clip `native` at 1 when no narration plays over its first 2 s.
- Narration: 6 s 12 to 15 words or none; 15 s 30 to 34; 30 s 62 to 70; 60 s
  125 to 140. Calm, close voice; sensory verbs; one benefit.
- Score: "Minimal warm electronic, soft plucked synth and a felt kick around
  100 BPM. Opens almost silent under the drop, adds one layer per scene,
  lands on a single held chord under the packshot. Instrumental." For
  fragrance: "slow felt piano and a low string pad around 70 BPM, a held
  note at the reveal".
- Mix: hook `native` 1; every narrated clip `mix` 0.3; packshot `mix` 0.3;
  score 0.3 to 0.35 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

**Pair 1: the hook drop**

Still:

```text
Premium product commercial still. Macro shot, 100mm macro lens, very shallow focus. The amber glass dropper of a serum bottle hangs just above the back of a woman's hand resting on wet white stone, one round drop at the tip. Soft light from above, a backlight making the serum glow honey-gold. Natural skin texture, fine pores. Palette: white, amber, sand. No text, no logo, no on-screen graphics.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera at macro distance. 0 to 1 s: the drop is already falling from the dropper tip. 1 to 3 s: it lands on the skin and spreads into a glossy bead that slowly flattens and stops. At 3 s the dropper lifts out of frame upward. The hand, the stone and the backlight stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one drop, one hand, nobody else in frame. SFX: a single soft drip on skin. Ambient noise: quiet room tone. No music. Clean frame, no text, no logo.
```

**Pair 2: application on the cheek**

Still:

```text
Premium product commercial still. Close-up, 85mm portrait lens, shallow focus. A woman in her early thirties with warm light-brown skin, short natural curls and a small mole above the left lip, eyes closed, a dab of thick white cream on her right cheekbone. Clamshell beauty light from above, a soft reflector under the chin. Natural skin texture, fine pores. Pale sand background. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera. 0 to 3 s: two fingertips of her right hand press the cream into the cheekbone in slow small circles. 3 to 4 s: the hand lowers out of frame and she opens her eyes; a soft sheen stays on the cheek. Her face, hair and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman and one hand in frame. SFX: a faint soft skin sound. Ambient noise: quiet room. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the packshot hold** (the scene opens on the imported packshot;
the still prompt is only the fallback when no packshot exists)

Still:

```text
Premium product commercial still. The serum bottle: a slim 30 ml amber glass bottle with rounded shoulders, a black rubber dropper bulb on a matte black collar, a plain cream label on the front, about the height of a hand. Standing on wet white stone, eye level, 85mm lens. Backlight through the glass, soft top light. Clean pale background, empty space on the upper third. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera. A soft band of light sweeps slowly across the glass from left to right over three seconds, the serum glowing as it passes, then the frame holds still. The bottle's shape, collar and label stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one bottle. Ambient noise: very quiet room tone. No music. Clean frame, no text, no logo.
```

**Second family (Seedance 1.5 Pro or 2.0).** Rewrite each motion prompt as
a short production note, subject first, with "already in motion from the
first frame", reference roles ("Reference 1 is the bottle's shape, glass,
collar and label layout only; Reference 2 is the woman's face and hair
only; ignore their backgrounds"), and add "natural skin texture, visible
pores, no smoothing" to CONTINUITY: Seedance tends to smooth skin.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Waxy, airbrushed skin | "natural skin texture, fine pores, a few freckles"; softer light, not stronger; Veo over Seedance for faces |
| Label melts during a turn | open on the packshot; push-in or light sweep only |
| The drop hangs, bounces or doubles | "exactly one drop"; land it within 1 s; trim to the landing |
| Cream turns yellow or grey | name the colour in the still ("thick white cream") and the light ("neutral daylight") |
| Fingers merge into the face | one hand, two fingertips, small circles; cut before the hand leaves |
| A second bottle in a mirror shot | keep mirrors out, or "the mirror shows only her face" |
| Bottle widens or grows droplets | "a rigid glass bottle, a solid unchanging form"; smaller move; trim |
| Veo adds a music bed | "No music." and `mix` 0.3 under narration |

## 9. Policy guards

- No AI "results": no before and after of acne, wrinkles, pigmentation,
  hair loss or lashes made by a model. Texture and ritual only. Real
  results only from the user's own photos with the person's consent.
- No medical or drug claims ("treats acne", "removes wrinkles", "heals")
  and no "clinically proven", "dermatologist tested" or percentages unless
  the user gives the claim and its source.
- AI models are models, not customers: no first-person result lines.
- No celebrity likeness, no other brand's products in frame.
