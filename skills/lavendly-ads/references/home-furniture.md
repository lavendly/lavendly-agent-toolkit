# Recipe: home and furniture

Lamps, chairs, sofas, bedding, kitchenware, rugs, decor, small home
appliances. The sale is how a room feels with the product in it: light,
texture, use. Shape, finish, colour and true scale must be exact; the room
is a set, not a listing.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: people furnishing a home on Instagram, Pinterest-style feeds,
  Meta, TikTok; product pages; catalogue films.
- The viewer should feel the material and picture the object in their own
  room at its real size.
- The ending: the product in its room at rest (the user's lifestyle photo)
  or on a clean background (the packshot), held 2 to 4 s, the call to
  action spoken, space for the logo and price card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the product doing its one thing with its sound: a lamp switching
  on, a duvet thrown open, a pan sizzling, a chair taking someone's weight.
  Or the texture in macro.
- Payoff: the room changed by the product (warmer, calmer, tidier), with a
  person at ease in it; then the product at rest, true to size.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 35mm wide at eye level for rooms, verticals straight; 50mm for
  the product in context; 85mm and 100mm macro for switches, weaves,
  grains, joints. Locked-off or slow push-ins; a slow pan for a reveal.
- Light: "soft window light from camera left, late afternoon" for day;
  "the lamp the only warm source, the rest of the room in cool dusk" for
  night; "warm tungsten pools on the floor, cool blue window". Light the
  room from what is in it.
- Colour: warm and natural; true material colours; "Palette: oak, linen,
  stone grey, brass" or the product's colours plus two neutrals.
- Scale: always a known object beside the product (a book, a mug, a hand,
  a person sitting) so size reads true.

## 5. Sound

- Native: the switch click, fabric, a mug on wood, rain on the window, room
  tone, a page turning.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 60 to 68; 60 s
  120 to 135. Warm, unhurried, one feeling and one material named.
- Score: "Warm felt piano and soft brushed drums around 84 BPM. Opens on
  the click, grows with the light, settles into a quiet loop under the
  reading shots, ends on a held chord under the lamp. Instrumental."
- Mix: switch and texture clips `native` 1 when no narration sits over
  them; room clips `mix` 0.3; score 0.3 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs

Examples: the user's own product, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1 (Veo): the switch**

Still:

```text
Premium product commercial still. Close-up at table height, 85mm lens, shallow focus. A table lamp with a matte stone-grey mushroom dome, a slim brass stem and a round brass base with a small brass switch, switched off, on a dark oak side table; a woman's hand rests beside the switch. Cool dusk light from a window on the left, the room dark. Palette: stone grey, brass, oak, dusk blue. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera. 0 to 1 s: the hand moves to the switch. 1 to 2 s: one finger presses it once. 2 to 4 s: warm light blooms under the dome and spreads across the oak; the hand withdraws out of frame. The lamp, the table and the window light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one lamp, one hand. SFX: a soft brass click. Ambient noise: quiet room tone. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2 (Veo): the room changes**

Still:

```text
Natural-light interior photograph. Wide shot at eye level, 35mm lens, vertical lines straight. A small reading corner at dusk: a linen armchair, a dark oak side table, a lit table lamp with a stone-grey dome and brass stem, a wool rug, a tall window with blue dusk sky. The lamp is the only warm source; the rest of the room cool and dim. Palette: dusk blue, linen, oak, warm amber. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in toward the armchair over the whole shot, then the camera settles. The warm pool of lamplight on the rug and the chair holds steady while the window sky darkens slightly. The room, the furniture and the lamp stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: soft rain on the window, room tone. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3 (Wan 2.7): the weave macro**

Still:

```text
Natural-light product photograph. Macro shot, 100mm lens, very shallow focus. The arm of an oatmeal linen sofa, a loose open weave with visible slubs, a folded wool throw at the edge of frame. Soft side light from the left raking across the weave. Palette: oatmeal, cream, soft grey. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
The camera slides slowly to the right along the sofa arm, the weave passing through the focus plane, then stops. Camera: slow move right, macro, close-up. Soft side light, warm colour tone, low contrast. The fabric and the throw keep their exact weave and colour. Nobody in frame. Sound: quiet room tone. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Wan 2.7) notes.** Wan maps literal category words:
light source (window, practical), light type (soft, side, rim), time of day
(dusk), shot size, lens (medium, wide, long-focus), colour tone (warm, low
contrast). Image to video wants motion plus camera, nothing more. Four
references reach it (three on the MCP path besides the first frame): the
product first, then the room.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The product changes size between shots | a scale object in every still; the product block names its size; the photo frame for the hero |
| Furniture legs bend, walls curve | "vertical lines straight"; slow moves; 35mm not 18mm for product rooms |
| Fabric pattern crawls | plain or tight weaves; macro slides of a few centimetres |
| Light switches on before the press | timed beats: "1 to 2 s presses, 2 to 4 s blooms" |
| Extra lamps or chairs appear | "exactly one lamp"; name every object in the room in the still |
| An unnamed shelf grows objects | say what is on every surface, or "an empty oak shelf" |

## 9. Policy guards

- True dimensions and colours: no shot that makes the product look larger
  or richer than it is; a scale object in frame.
- Material claims ("solid oak", "100 percent linen", "sustainably sourced")
  only as the user gives them.
- Rooms are sets: never imply a real customer's home or a real address.
- No other brands' furniture designs named in prompts.
