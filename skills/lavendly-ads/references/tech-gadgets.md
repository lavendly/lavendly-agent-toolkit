# Recipe: tech and gadgets

Headphones, speakers, chargers, phones and cases, cameras, wearables, small
appliances. The sale is precision and the one thing it does better: the
fold, the click, the silence. Edges, finishes and proportions must be exact;
screens and lettering stay blank.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: buyers comparing options on TikTok, Reels and YouTube; launch
  pages; retail product pages.
- The viewer should understand the one feature by seeing it work, and the
  object's quality from its edges and materials.
- The ending: the product at rest on a plinth or in a hand, the packshot
  held 2 to 4 s, the call to action spoken, negative space for the user's
  logo and price card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the mechanism in action with its sound (a fold click, a magnetic
  snap, a cap pop, a power-on glow), or the problem the product removes (a
  tangle of cables, a noisy room). Frame 1 is already moving.
- Payoff: the feature seen working (the room going quiet, the cable
  snapping home, the phone charging without a cable shown by a small light
  on the base), then the product at rest.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 100mm macro on ports, buttons, hinges and textures; 85mm for
  profile reveals; slow orbits of 45 to 90 degrees on the plinth; locked-off
  for packshots and mechanisms. Never a full orbit.
- Light, dark style: "low-key studio, two long vertical strip lights drawing
  reflections along the edges, a thin cool rim light tracing the chamfers,
  black background". Light, minimal style: "high-key white seamless, broad
  soft light from above, a soft contact shadow".
- Screens: "a plain dark glass screen, switched off", or the screen turned
  away, or a soft glow seen from the side with no content. A small status
  light is fine: "one small white light on the base".
- Colour: neutral to cool; deep blacks; "Palette: graphite, black, one cool
  white highlight", or the product's own colour plus greys.

## 5. Sound

- Native: clicks, snaps, button presses, a low whoosh on an orbit; for audio
  products the ambience that disappears is the story.
- Narration: 6 s 12 to 15 words or none; 15 s 30 to 34; 30 s 62 to 70; 60 s
  125 to 140. Direct, specific, one feature named in plain words. No specs
  unless the user gives them.
- Score: "Minimal electronic around 116 BPM, tight kick and a sub pulse,
  one rising synth line. Opens on the click, drops to near silence when the
  feature works, returns full under the packshot. Instrumental."
- Mix: mechanism clips `native` 1 when no narration sits over them; the
  in-use noise drop clip `native` (its own ambience is the effect) with the
  score ducked; narrated clips `mix` 0.3; packshot `mix` 0.2.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Screen shows nonsense UI | "a plain dark glass screen, switched off"; turn it away; real UI only from the user's own recording |
| Extra buttons, ports or lenses appear | open on the packshot; "keeps its exact buttons and ports"; smaller orbit |
| Edges wobble during an orbit | 45 degrees, not 180; "the camera settles and holds"; trim before the wobble |
| Logo or model number appears on the product | never name the brand; "unmarked surface" on the parts that carry print |
| Cable bends like rubber or passes through the port | one straight insertion, the contact named; cut on the click |
| Hand grips through the object | one hand, one grip, the contact named; or a hands-free shot |
| A glowing screen floods the frame | "a soft glow from the side, no content visible" |

## 9. Policy guards

- No specs, battery hours, noise reduction figures, charging speeds or
  "fastest" claims unless the user gives them with a source.
- No interface that the product does not have; real screens only from the
  user's recordings.
- No named competitors, no other brands' devices in frame, no logos sent to
  the model.
- People are AI actors in a scene, not reviewers.
