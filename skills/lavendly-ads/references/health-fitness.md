# Recipe: health and fitness (within policy)

Gym gear, activewear, home equipment, shaker bottles and drink powders,
yoga and recovery tools, fitness and wellness apps. The sale is the feeling
of doing it: effort, rhythm, recovery. This category carries the strictest
policy: the ad shows the product and the activity, never a body result, a
health outcome or a promise about the viewer's body.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: people building a habit on TikTok, Reels, YouTube; gym and
  studio screens; product pages.
- The viewer should feel the movement and see the product in honest use.
- The ending: the product at rest after the session (the kettlebell on the
  mat, the bottle on the bench), held 2 to 3 s, the call to action spoken
  ("Start tomorrow morning."), space for the logo and offer card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: a controlled movement already in progress (a lift, a step-up, a
  stretch reaching its end), or the product's sound (a band snapping taut,
  the shaker lid). Never a body "before" shot.
- Payoff: calm after effort (breath, sitting in the sun, a quiet smile),
  and the gear at rest. The feeling, not a physique.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 35mm medium wide for whole-body moves (feet in frame), 85mm for
  faces and grips, 100mm macro for chalk and textures, low angle for
  strength, tracking alongside for carries. Locked-off for any rep: a
  moving camera on a moving body warps limbs.
- Light: "cold dawn light through a half-open garage door, one warm bulb
  overhead"; "hard backlight through dust, a rim along the shoulders"; for
  studios "large soft window light, pale wood floor". Avoid "glistening".
- Colour: desaturated with one accent (the product colour); "Palette:
  concrete grey, black, one orange accent".
- Bodies: ordinary athletic builds, sweat as small beads at the temple,
  clothes with real creases; always "feet flat on the floor", "both hands on
  the handle", "one controlled movement at a steady pace".

## 5. Sound

- Native: breath, a kettlebell set down on rubber, chalk clap, the shaker
  lid, sneakers on concrete.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 60 to 68; 60 s
  120 to 135. Second person, present, about the habit and the feeling, never
  about weight, shape or outcomes.
- Score: "Driving minimal electronic around 124 BPM, a heavy kick and a
  breathy synth pad. Opens on the lift with a single hit, builds through the
  reps, drops to the pad for the recovery shot, ends on one hit under the
  gear. Instrumental."
- Mix: lift and set-down clips `native` 1 with no narration over them;
  rep clips `mix` 0.4 (breath under the score); score 0.4 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Kling)

**Pair 1: the lift**

Still:

```text
Natural-light fitness photograph. Medium shot, low angle, 35mm lens. A woman in her thirties with a dark braid, a grey sleeveless top and black leggings stands with feet planted on a black rubber mat, bent at the hips, both hands on the handle of a matte black kettlebell on the floor between her feet. A garage gym at dawn, cold light through a half-open door behind her. Palette: concrete grey, black, cold blue. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
From the opening frame, the woman lifts the kettlebell from the floor to her chest in one controlled movement at a steady pace, standing tall, elbows tucked, then holds it there. The camera stays locked-off at a low angle. Her feet stay flat on the mat; both hands stay on the handle. Her face, clothes and the garage stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman, one kettlebell. Sound: a breath out, the soft scrape of the bell leaving the mat, garage room tone. Clean frame, no text.
```

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Limbs bend wrong or swap mid-rep | locked-off camera, one rep, steady pace, feet named as planted; medium-wide so the body is whole |
| The weight floats or changes size | "the same kettlebell, solid cast iron"; the product block verbatim |
| Ball, band or rope teleports | avoid fast contact moves; show the controlled phase only |
| Faces change between shots | one face block verbatim; a face reference within the cap |
| Glossy plastic skin | "small sweat beads at the temple, natural skin texture" |
| The clock or app screen shows nonsense | a plain clock with no numerals; screens off or away |

## 9. Policy guards

- No before and after bodies, no weight, size or measurement claims, no
  "results in thirty days", no AI physique presented as an outcome.
- Nothing that implies the viewer's body is flawed; no body-shaming
  framing; no extreme thinness or muscle as an aspiration.
- Supplements: no disease, treatment, immunity or weight-loss claims;
  ingredients and benefits only as the user gives them with their basis;
  follow the placement's health rules for the user's market.
- Safe form only: no dangerous lifts, no exercise while injured, adults
  only.
- Apps: no medical advice, no diagnosis, no screen content invented.
