# Recipe: fashion and apparel

Clothing, shoes, bags, eyewear, hats. The sale is fit, fabric and how it
moves on a body. The garment's cut, colour, pattern and hardware must be
exact; the model is an AI actor, not a real person, and stays the same
person in every shot.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: shoppers on Reels, TikTok and Meta feed; e-commerce product
  pages; drop announcements.
- The viewer should feel the fabric and see the fit in motion.
- The ending: the full look standing still, or the garment flat-lay or
  packshot, held 2 to 3 s, the call to action spoken, space for the logo and
  price card.

## 2. Structure by length

Veo generate 4. "Photo" means the scene opens on the user's on-model photo;

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the garment moving on a body in frame 1 (a walk toward the lens, a
  coat swinging off a shoulder, a skirt catching the wind, a sneaker landing
  a step). One movement, no spin over 180 degrees.
- Payoff: the full look still, the drape settled, then the price spoken.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 50mm full shots at eye level for fit; 85mm for fabric and
  hardware; 100mm macro for weave and stitching; low angle 35mm for
  stature; camera leading backward in front of a walk; slow arcs of 30
  degrees. Locked-off full shots keep a set of looks consistent.
- Light: "flat overcast daylight, soft shadowless light" for true colour;
  "hard low sun from the side, crisp long shadows" for summer; "large soft
  key from camera left on a pale grey seamless, a soft floor shadow" for
  e-commerce.
- Colour: let the garment lead; muted backgrounds; "Kodak Portra film
  colours, soft warm pastels" for editorial; "neutral white balance,
  true-to-life colours" for e-commerce. Palette anchors from the garment
  ("Palette: camel, white, concrete grey").

## 5. Sound

- Native: footsteps on concrete or paper backdrop, fabric rustle, wind, a
  zip, a buckle.
- Narration: often none (music-led). When used: 6 s up to 10 words; 15 s
  20 to 30; 30 s 45 to 65; 60 s 100 to 130. Fabric, cut and the one detail.
- Score: "Confident minimal house around 120 BPM, dry kick, a warm bass and
  a clipped synth stab. Opens with the first footstep, a stab on each cut
  of the walk, strips to the kick under the macro, lands on a held chord
  under the final look. Instrumental."
- Mix: walk clips `mix` 0.4 (footsteps under the score); macro `native` 1
  for the rustle when no narration; final look `mix` 0.3; score 0.5 to 0.7
  in a music-led edit, 0.3 with narration and ducking on.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Seedance)

**Pair 1: the walk toward the lens** (opens on the user's on-model photo
when there is one; the still prompt is the fallback)

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Full shot, eye level, 50mm lens. A tall woman in her late twenties with a short black bob walks toward camera in a long camel wool coat over a white t-shirt, dark grey wide trousers and white leather sneakers, on an empty concrete rooftop under a flat overcast sky. Soft shadowless daylight. Palette: camel, white, concrete grey. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
The woman in the camel coat, already walking toward camera from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 5 s, four steady steps at walking pace, the coat hem swinging with each step, the wind lifting the collar on the third step. CAMERA: leads backward in front of her at her pace, keeping her full figure in frame. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the coat's cut, colour, buttons and length only; reference 2 is her face and hair only; ignore their backgrounds. Exactly one woman, nobody else. AUDIO: footsteps on concrete, city wind, no music. ENDING STATE: mid-stride, full figure centred.
```

**Pair 2: the fabric macro**

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Macro shot, 100mm lens, very shallow focus. The lapel of a camel wool coat with a fine diagonal twill weave, edge stitching in matching thread, one round brown horn button. Soft side light from the left raking across the weave. Palette: camel, brown, cream. No text, no labels.
```

Motion:

```text
The camel wool lapel, the camera already sliding from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 5 s, the camera slides slowly to the right along the lapel, the weave passing through the focus plane, ending on the horn button. CAMERA: macro slide of a few centimetres, no other move. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the coat's fabric, colour and button only. The fabric, stitching and button stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. AUDIO: a faint fabric rustle, no music. ENDING STATE: the button sharp at frame centre.
```

**Pair 3: the turn to profile**

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Full shot, low angle, 35mm lens. The same woman with the short black bob stands still facing camera in the long camel wool coat, white t-shirt, dark grey wide trousers and white sneakers, on the concrete rooftop, the coat hanging straight. Flat overcast daylight. Palette: camel, white, concrete grey. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
The woman in the camel coat, already beginning to turn from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 3 s, she turns slowly to her left until she stands in profile, the coat swinging out and settling; 3 to 5 s, she holds still. CAMERA: slow arc of about 30 degrees to the right, then settles. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the coat only; reference 2 is her face and hair only; the coat, her face and the rooftop stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman. AUDIO: a fabric swish, wind, no music. ENDING STATE: profile, the drape still.
```

**Second family (Kling v3 Pro).** Treat the opening frame as the anchor;
"From the opening frame, the woman walks toward camera"; plain verbs ("the
camera moves backward at walking pace"); one keep line ("the coat keeps its
exact cut, colour and buttons"); a "Sound:" line. Two references at most.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pattern, print or logo changes mid-walk | open on the user's photo; plain fabrics hold best; avoid print close to the lens |
| Garment length or cut drifts | the garment named identically in every still; the reference role sentence; slower walk |
| The model's face changes between looks | the face reference wired to every clip; the face block verbatim |
| Outfit change transitions morph | hard cut between looks, never a morph |
| Spins tear the garment | at most a turn to profile; no spin over 180 degrees |
| Legs swap or feet slide | full shot at walking pace, four steps, "feet planted with each step" |
| A second person walks in | "exactly one woman, nobody else"; an empty location named |

## 9. Policy guards

- The garment shown is the garment sold: colour, length, fabric and fit as
  in the user's photos. Say when a scene shows an approximation.
- AI models are AI actors: no real models' or celebrities' likeness, no
  first-person fit claims ("true to size", "fits my curves") from an AI
  person.
- No other brands' logos, monograms or recognisable designs in prompts.
- Sizing and material claims ("100 percent wool") only as the user gives.
