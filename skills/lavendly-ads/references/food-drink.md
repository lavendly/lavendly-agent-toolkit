# Recipe: food and drink

Coffee, soft drinks, beer and wine (within policy), snacks, packaged food,
sauces, a restaurant's signature item. The sale is appetite: steam,
condensation, the pour, the crunch, the pull. The pack must be exact; the
food must look like what the buyer gets.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: hungry scrollers on TikTok and Reels, delivery app listings,
  YouTube pre-roll, in-store screens.
- The viewer should taste it: temperature, texture, sound.
- The ending: the hero serving beside the pack, or the pack alone, held 2
  to 3 s, the call to action spoken ("Grab one cold."), clean space for the
  user's logo and price card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the most appetising motion with its sound at 0 s: a pour over ice,
  fizz rising, a cheese pull, a sizzle, a crunch with crumbs falling, steam
  curling. Never a closed pack first.
- Payoff: the satisfaction shown by the result (an empty plate, a glass set
  down half empty, a slow breath out), never by a mouth chewing in close-up.
  Then the pack.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: table height for drinks, top-down for plates and flat lays, 45
  degrees for burgers and stacks, 100mm macro for texture, locked-off for
  pours. One pour, one path, per clip.
- Light: "side backlight at about 100 degrees from camera making the steam
  and the liquid glow, a large soft key at 45 degrees, a small fill card";
  for dark moody food "one window light from the side, black flags, deep
  shadows"; for fresh food "bright soft daylight on white marble".
- Colour: warm and natural, never blue; matte surfaces; "Palette: espresso
  brown, amber, cream" or "tomato red, golden yellow, charcoal".
- Liquids and steam: "slow motion", "steam rising and curling slowly in the
  backlight", "fine beads of condensation, a few running down".

## 5. Sound

- Native: pour, fizz, ice clink, sizzle, crunch, the can tab. These are the
  hook: keep that clip `native` 1 with no narration over its first 2 s.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 60 to 68; 60 s
  120 to 135. Short, sensory, one benefit; the name said once early.
- Score: "Laid-back funk guitar and a light shaker around 110 BPM, warm and
  unhurried. Opens on the pour sound, builds with a bass line from scene 3,
  ends on a short guitar sting under the packshot. Instrumental."
- Mix: pour and crunch clips `native` 1; narrated clips `mix` 0.4 (the fizz
  under the voice helps); packshot `mix` 0.3; score 0.3 with ducking.

Premium product commercial still. Medium close-up, 50mm lens, low angle at table height. ```text

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Liquid moves like jelly, pours from nowhere | one pour, one source named ("from the top of frame"), slow motion, under 4 s |
| Steam frozen or painted | "steam rising and curling slowly in the backlight"; backlight named |
| Food morphs into another dish | one action per clip; the dish named the same way in both prompts |
| Bites and chewing look wrong | never a mouth chewing close; show the lift, the empty plate, the breath out |
| Knife does not cut | show the result, not the slicing physics; cut away on contact |
| Can label garbled | open on the packshot; elsewhere "unmarked can" or label turned away |
| Too many ice cubes appear | "three ice cubes", a count in the still and the motion |
| Veo adds music over the pour | "No music." and the pour clip `native` |

## 9. Policy guards

- The food shown is the food sold: portion, toppings and colour match the
  real product; no AI dish presented as a restaurant's actual plate unless
  the scene opens on the restaurant's own photo.
- Nutrition, health, "natural", "organic" or "sugar free" claims only as the
  user gives them, with their basis.
- Alcohol: people clearly adults over 25, no drinking linked to driving,
  sport performance, social success or excess; follow the placement's
  alcohol rules and the user's market age limits.
- No other brands' packs in frame.
