# Recipe: automotive

Cars, motorbikes, e-bikes, scooters, accessories, dealership offers. The
sale is design, motion and a feeling of place. The vehicle's body, lights
and wheels must be exact; badges, plates and dashboards stay blank; driving
stays lawful.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: buyers in consideration on YouTube, Meta and TikTok;
  dealership pages; launch films.
- The viewer should feel the design in light and the motion on the road.
- The ending: the vehicle still in a hero three-quarter view (the user's
  photo), held 2 to 4 s, the call to action spoken ("Book a test drive."),
  space for the logo and offer card.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: a light switching on, a door closing with its sound, a wheel
  starting to roll, reflections sweeping the body. Never the badge.
- Payoff: the car at rest in a place the viewer wants to be, the design
  readable in one three-quarter view, then held.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: low front three-quarter at 35mm; tracking alongside at a constant
  speed; aerial top-down on bends; slow truck along the side for
  reflections; locked-off hero. Never a full orbit of the car.
- Light: studio "a large overhead softbox and long strip reflections running
  the length of the body, black floor"; night "sodium orange ceiling lights
  reflecting along the bonnet, wet black asphalt"; location "golden hour, low
  warm sun from behind, a warm line along the roof"; "blue hour, deep blue
  sky, the headlights on".
- Colour: deep contrast, clean reflections; "Palette: bottle green, sodium
  orange, black" or the paint colour plus the sky.
- Motion words: "rolling at a steady speed", "wheels turning forward
  smoothly", "the road surface streaking past, the car body steady".

## 5. Sound

- Native: engine start, the door close, tyres on wet road, wind; for an
  electric vehicle a low hum and tyre noise.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 60 to 68; 60 s
  120 to 135. Calm, low, few words; the design and the feeling.
- Score: "Slow-building analogue synth pulse around 96 BPM, low bass, a
  distant guitar line. Opens on the light switching on, rises with the road,
  lands on a held chord at the lookout. Instrumental."
- Mix: ignition and door clips `native` 1; road clips `mix` 0.4 (tyre and
  wind under the score); packshot `mix` 0.2; score 0.35 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

**Pair 1: the car park reflections**

```text
Premium automotive commercial still. Low front three-quarter view, 35mm lens, camera at bumper height. A dark green two-door coupe parked in an empty multi-storey car park at night, unmarked plain number plate, sodium orange ceiling lights reflecting along the bonnet, wet black concrete, a light mist. Palette: bottle green, sodium orange, black. No text, no logo, no badges.
```

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wheels spin backward or stand still | "wheels turning forward smoothly at a steady speed"; constant tracking speed |
| Body panels morph during a move | a slow truck, never an orbit; open the hero on the photo |
| Badge or plate garbled | "unmarked plain number plate", "no badges"; never a badge close-up |
| Dashboard shows nonsense | "a blank dark dashboard, screens off" |
| The car drifts across lanes | "keeping it in the centre of frame", one road named |
| Two cars appear | "exactly one car on the road" |
| Road geometry changes in aerials | slow advance, short clip, trim before a bend reshapes |

## 9. Policy guards

- Lawful driving only: no speeding on public roads, no skids or racing
  lines in traffic, no phone use while driving, seatbelts on. Track or
  closed-road scenes say so.
- No speed, range, fuel or price claims unless the user gives them with
  their basis.
- The vehicle shown is the vehicle sold: trim, wheels and colour from the
  user's photos.
- No other makes' badges or recognisable designs in prompts.
