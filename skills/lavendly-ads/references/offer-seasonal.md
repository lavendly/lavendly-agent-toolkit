# Recipe: offer and seasonal promo

Sales, discount codes, bundles, free shipping, holiday gifting, back to
school, summer drops, Black Friday and end-of-season. The sale is the offer
itself, made concrete by the product in a moment of the season. Offers are
made fast and often, so cost matters; the terms must be exact.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: existing followers and warm audiences on Meta, TikTok,
  Instagram Stories, YouTube; email and site banners as video.
- The viewer should know what is offered, on what, until when, and how to
  get it, from the pictures and captions alone.
- The ending: the packshot in seasonal dress, held 3 s or more, the offer
  and deadline spoken once more, space for the user's price card and code.

## 2. The offer card

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| The offer | exactly the user's words: "twenty percent off", "buy two, get one free" |
| On what | the product or range, named as the user names it |
| Until when | the user's date; spoken as words ("until Sunday the twelfth") |
| How | the code, the link, the store; a code is spelled out letter by letter in speech only if short |
| Terms | what the user says must be shown; the caption carries "terms apply" when they say so |

The hook and the call to action both carry the offer: the offer is the
promise. Urgency only when real: no countdown, "last chance" or "only
three left" unless true.

## 3. Structure by length

Gen is the length to generate on Seedance 2.0 Fast (5 s); on Veo generate
4. "Packshot" means the scene opens on the imported packshot.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | 3 to 6 | medium, 85mm | locked-off | packshot in seasonal dress, the deadline spoken | 3 | 5, packshot |

**60 s**: rarely right for an offer. Use the 30 s and offer a 6 s bumper
and a 15 s cut-down from the same clips (trims and order only, no new
clips); render each. If the user insists on 60 s: two products, each
with the 30 s middle (season, use, material, wrapping, giving), 14 shots,
the offer in the first and last line.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the product arriving into a seasonal frame (a box sliding across a
  table, a bag set down on snow) with the offer spoken in the first 2 s.
- Payoff: the offer restated over the packshot, the deadline last, held.

## 5. Camera, light, colour by season

| Season | Light | Palette | Set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winter holidays | "warm string lights out of focus behind, cool blue window light from the left" | deep green, warm gold, cream, cranberry | wrapping paper, wool, frost on glass, pine |
| Black Friday, end of season | "low-key studio, one warm spotlight on the product, a dark background" | black, warm white, one accent colour | a plinth, the product alone |
| Summer | "hard midday sun, short crisp shadows" | sky blue, sand, white | beach towel, ice, sunglasses |
| Back to school | "bright morning window light" | primary colours softened | desk, notebooks with blank covers |
| Spring | "soft overcast daylight" | pale green, blush, white | blossom branches, linen |
| Valentine's | "warm candlelight from the right" | deep red, blush, gold | a small table for two |

Camera: 50mm for moments, 85mm for packshots and hands, slow push-ins,
locked-off holds. Seasonal props stay generic: no trademarked characters,
no other brands' holiday packaging.

## 6. Sound

- Native: paper, ribbon, a box set down, snow underfoot, ice in a glass.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words (the offer, the deadline); 15 s 28 to 32;
  30 s 60 to 68. The offer in the first line and the last line, the same
  words.
- Score: seasonal but generic, "Warm bells and a light upright bass around
  104 BPM, cosy and bright, a small lift on the giving shot, ends on a
  sleigh-bell button" (winter); "Bright plucked guitar and claps around 116
  BPM" (summer). Instrumental; no recognisable holiday song.
- Mix: narrated clips `mix` 0.3; the box slide `native` 1 when no
  narration sits on its first second; score 0.35 with ducking.
- Captions on (`caption` for sound-off feed, `reels` or `tiktok` for
  vertical): the offer is the line the muted viewer must read.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Seedance 2.0 Fast)

Examples: the user's own product, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1: the box slides into frame**

Still:

```text
Premium seasonal product still. Medium shot at table height, 50mm lens. A dark oak table in a cosy room at night, warm string lights out of focus behind, a cool blue window on the left with frost at the edges; the right half of the table is empty. Palette: deep green, warm gold, cream. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
A small cream gift box tied with a green ribbon, already sliding into frame from the right from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 2 s, the box slides across the oak and stops at the centre; 2 to 5 s, the string lights twinkle softly behind, nothing else moves. CAMERA: locked-off, no movement. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the box's shape, colour and ribbon only; the table, the lights and the window stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one box. AUDIO: a soft wooden slide, a faint room hum, no music. ENDING STATE: the box still at the centre of the table.
```

**Pair 2: the product in the season (a scarf)**

Still:

```text
Natural-light seasonal photograph. Close-up, 85mm lens, shallow focus. A woman's gloved hands wrap a thick oatmeal wool scarf around her neck on a snowy street at blue hour, warm shop windows glowing out of focus behind, a few snowflakes in the air. Palette: oatmeal, deep blue, warm gold. No text, no signs, no logos.
```

Motion:

```text
The oatmeal wool scarf, the hands already wrapping it from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 3 s, the hands bring the scarf once around the neck and tuck the end; 3 to 5 s, the hands lower out of frame and snowflakes drift past. CAMERA: slow push-in toward the scarf, then settles. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the scarf's knit, colour and fringe only; ignore its background; the street and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one person, one scarf. AUDIO: wool rustle, soft snowy street ambience, no music. ENDING STATE: the scarf wrapped, still.
```

**Pair 3: the packshot hold** (opens on the packshot in seasonal dress the
user supplies, or the plain packshot)

Motion:

```text
The product on the oak table, still from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 4 s, a soft band of warm light sweeps across it from left to right, then the frame holds. CAMERA: locked-off. CONTINUITY: the product's shape, finish and label stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one product. AUDIO: faint room tone, no music. ENDING STATE: the product evenly lit, still.
```

**Second family (Veo 3.1).** Plain sentences, camera first, timed beats,
`SFX:` and `Ambient noise:` lines, "No music.", 16:9 or 9:16 only.

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| A price or "SALE" appears in the frame | no words, numbers or signs in prompts; the offer is spoken and captioned |
| Gift paper shows invented patterns or letters | "plain cream paper", "solid green ribbon" |
| Snow or lights swamp the product | name the product's position and size; snow "a few flakes" |
| Holiday characters appear | generic props named; no character names |
| The seasonal set differs between shots | one set line pasted verbatim per scene |
| The deadline is misheard | spell the date out in words; repeat it last |

## 10. Policy guards

- The offer, price, code, dates and terms exactly as the user gives them;
  never invent scarcity, a countdown or a "was" price.
- No recognisable holiday songs, characters or other brands' seasonal
  campaigns.
- Gifting scenes cast adults; no children as purchasers.
