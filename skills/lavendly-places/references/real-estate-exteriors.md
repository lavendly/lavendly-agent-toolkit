# Recipe: real estate and architecture exteriors

Listing films led by the outside of a home, building and development
films, architecture portfolios, and concept films for unbuilt projects.
The sale is arrival: the first view, the setting, the light on the
facade, the dusk glow. A real property is shown from its own photos, and
nothing about it is improved.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: buyers and renters on listing sites, Instagram, YouTube;
  developers' investors; an architect's clients.
- The viewer should know what the place looks like from the street, how it
  sits in its plot and its neighbourhood, and how it feels at dusk.
- The ending: the twilight exterior (a twilight photo) or the front
  elevation held 3 to 4 s, the call to action spoken ("Book a viewing this
  week."), space for the address and agent card the user adds.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the single best feature first, moving slowly into view: the view
  over water, the garden, the double-height glass, the setting from the
  air. Never the agent's logo first.
- Payoff: the twilight exterior, every window warm, held. The feeling of
  coming home.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 24mm architectural lens, verticals straight, camera at about 1.5
  m for elevations; slow pedestal ups over hedges and walls; slow drone
  advances and 45-degree orbits; lateral trucks along facades; 85mm slides
  on materials. Never a whip pan; never a full orbit.
- Light: "soft sun from the right, a clear pale sky" for day; "blue hour,
  deep blue sky, every window glowing warm, path lights on" for twilight
  (only from a twilight photo for a real property); "bright overcast,
  even light, no harsh shadows" for architecture details.
- Colour: neutral white balance, true colours, a clean sky, no heavy grade.
  "Palette: white brick, black steel, green lawn, pale sky."
- Architecture concept films may grade more ("warm concrete, low sun,
  long shadows"), labelled as concept.

## 5. Sound

- Native: birds, a breeze in trees, distant traffic, gravel underfoot,
  evening crickets.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 55 to 68; 60
  s 115 to 135. Calm, features and feeling, true numbers spelled out
  ("three bedrooms", "a south-facing garden" only if true).
- Score: "Light acoustic guitar and soft piano around 96 BPM, warm and
  unhurried. Opens on a single guitar figure over the aerial, adds piano at
  the facade, lifts gently in the garden, ends on a held chord at
  twilight. Instrumental."
- Mix: every clip `mix` 0.3 under narration (birds under the voice);
  twilight clip `mix` 0.4 (crickets carry the end); score 0.3 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

Examples: the user's own place, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1: the facade rising over the hedge** (photo scene; the still is
for a concept film)

Still:

```text
Architectural photograph. Wide front elevation from the street, 24mm lens, camera at hedge height, vertical lines straight. A modern two-storey white brick house with black steel window frames, a flat roof, a pale oak front door, a low clipped hedge across the lower edge of the frame. Soft sun from the right, a clear pale sky. Palette: white brick, black steel, green, pale blue. No text, no signs, no house number.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow pedestal up from hedge height to first-floor height over the whole shot, then the camera settles. The hedge slides down out of the lower frame as the facade rises into full view. Leaves on the hedge move slightly in a breeze. The house, its windows, roof and door stay exactly as in the opening frame; vertical lines stay straight. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: birds, a light breeze, distant traffic. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: the twilight hold** (photo scene from a twilight photo)

Still:

```text
Architectural photograph. Straight-on front elevation, 24mm lens, vertical lines straight. A modern two-storey white brick house with black window frames at blue hour, every window glowing warm, small path lights along the front walk, a deep blue sky. Clean colours. No text, no signs, no house number.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera on a tripod. The path lights flicker on one by one from the gate toward the door over the first two seconds, then everything holds still. The house, its windows and the sky stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: evening crickets, a distant dog. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the aerial advance** (photo scene from the user's aerial photo,
or a concept still)

Still:

```text
Architectural photograph. High aerial view, 24mm lens, looking down at about thirty degrees. A low timber house with a dark roof on a green plot at the edge of a still lake, a gravel drive curving to the road, a line of birch trees on the left. Soft late afternoon sun from the right. Palette: dark timber, green, silver water. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
High aerial drone shot slowly advancing toward the house over the whole shot, height steady. The birch leaves shimmer; small ripples cross the lake. The house, the drive and the shoreline stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: wind, a distant bird. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Wan 2.7).** Motion plus camera, then literal words:
"Slow pedestal up, wide shot, wide lens, eye level. Soft sunlight, side
light, daytime, neutral colour tone, low contrast. The house keeps its
exact facade, windows and roof." For Hailuo 2.3 (16:9, silent): the
description then `[Pedestal up]`, one command per beat.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Windows multiply or move on the facade | photo scene; small move; trim before the change |
| Verticals lean | "vertical lines stay straight"; pedestal and trucks, not tilts |
| The roofline or chimney changes in an aerial | slow advance, 4 s, no orbit past 45 degrees |
| The sky turns orange | "a clear pale sky"; neutral white balance |
| A house number or street sign is invented | "no house number, no signs" |
| Neighbouring houses change | keep them out of frame or soft; the photo carries them |

## 9. Policy guards

- A real listing shows only what is there: no added pool, extension, view,
  landscaping, season or sky that misrepresents it. Twilight only from a
  twilight photo. Staging or concept labelled by the user ("virtually
  staged", "artist's impression").
- Price, size, bedrooms, school catchments and travel times only as the
  user gives them.
- No neighbours' faces or number plates; no identifiable people without
  consent.
- Unbuilt projects are concept films, labelled, never presented as built.
