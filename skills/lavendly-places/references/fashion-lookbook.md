# Recipe: fashion lookbook

A collection shown look by look: seasonal lookbooks, capsule drops,
stylists' edits, resale collections, e-commerce look films. The sale is
the collection as a whole: one world, one model, one rhythm, each look
readable for three seconds. Garments must be the real garments; the
model is one AI actor who stays the same person across every look.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: shoppers and buyers on Instagram, TikTok, the brand's site,
  wholesale presentations.
- The viewer should see every look's silhouette, colour and one detail,
  and feel the collection's place and mood.
- The ending: the strongest look again, or the whole cast of looks as a
  final still frame the user supplies, held 3 s; the call to action spoken
  or left to the caption; space for the logo and collection name.

## 2. The look grid

Before shots, list the looks: look number, garments (exact words from the
user's product names, no brand names), the photo that shows it (the
user's on-model photo opens that look's scene; without one the scene is an
approximation and says so), and the one detail to show.

| Look | Garments | Opens on | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | oversized black wool blazer, white wide-leg trousers, black loafers | the user's photo | the blazer's shoulder line |
| 2 | rust linen dress, tan leather sandals | text-made (approximation) | the dress hem in the wind |

## 3. Structure by length

Gen is the length to generate on Seedance (5 s); on Veo generate 4.
Same set, same framing family, same light for every look, so the cuts read
as rhythm, not as different films.

| 2 | 3 to 6 | full shot, 50mm | locked-off | look 2, same mark, the same turn | 3 | 5 |

**60 s** (a narrative lookbook): the model moves through three places
(gallery, street, rooftop), 10 to 12 looks, a fabric detail every third
shot, one bridging shot per change of place, the strongest look last.
18 to 20 shots.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the strongest look first, moving (a walk to the mark, a coat off
  the shoulder, a skirt catching the wind), cut on the first beat.
- Payoff: the final look still, held; the rhythm stops and the collection
  lands.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: locked-off full shots at 50mm on one mark for every look (the
  rhythm comes from the cuts, not the camera); 85mm and 100mm for details;
  a leading-backward track only for a narrative lookbook. Outfit changes
  are hard cuts on the beat, never morphs.
- Light: "large soft key from camera left on a pale grey seamless, a soft
  floor shadow" (studio); "flat overcast daylight" (true colour outdoors);
  "hard low sun with crisp shadows" (summer).
- Colour: editorial, restrained backgrounds; "Kodak Portra film colours"
  or "neutral white balance, true-to-life colours" for e-commerce.
  Palette anchors from the collection.

## 6. Sound

- Native: footsteps on the floor, fabric swish, a buckle.
- Narration: usually none. When used: one line per three looks, 15 s up to
  15 words, 30 s up to 30.
- Score: "Minimal deep house around 122 BPM, dry kick, warm bass, a muted
  chord stab every bar. A cut on every bar line (about 2 s) or every two
  bars, a breakdown under the details, the kick drops out under the final
  look." Instrumental.
- Mix: look clips `mix` 0.3 (footsteps under the beat); score 0.8 to 0.9
  for a music-led edit; captions off unless narrated.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Seedance)

**Pair 1: the studio look** (opens on the user's on-model photo when there
is one)

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Full shot, eye level, 50mm lens. A tall woman in her twenties with cropped bleached hair wears an oversized black wool blazer, white wide-leg trousers and black leather loafers, standing on a chalk mark on a pale grey seamless background. Large soft key light from camera left, a soft shadow on the floor. Palette: black, white, pale grey. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
The woman in the black blazer, already stepping forward from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 2 s, two steps forward to the mark; 2 to 5 s, she turns slowly to her left to show the profile, the blazer swinging and settling. CAMERA: locked-off, full figure in frame. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the blazer and trousers only; reference 2 is her face and hair only; ignore their backgrounds; the clothes, her face and the backdrop stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman. AUDIO: footsteps on paper backdrop, fabric swish, no music. ENDING STATE: profile, still.
```

**Pair 2: the dune look**

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Wide shot, low angle, 35mm lens. The same woman with cropped bleached hair in a flowing rust-orange linen dress and tan leather sandals stands on a sand dune at golden hour, long shadows across the ripples, a pale blue sky. Palette: rust, sand, pale blue. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
The woman in the rust linen dress, the wind already lifting the fabric from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 5 s, the dress billows and settles twice; she turns her head toward the sun. CAMERA: slow arc of about 30 degrees from left to right around her, then holds. CONTINUITY: reference 1 is the dress's colour, fabric and length only; reference 2 is her face and hair only; the dress, her face and the dune stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman, nobody else on the dune. AUDIO: wind across sand, no music. ENDING STATE: three-quarter view, the dress still.
```

**Pair 3: the weave detail**

Still:

```text
Editorial fashion photograph. Macro shot, 100mm lens, very shallow focus. The lapel of an oversized black wool blazer, a coarse twill weave, a single matte black button, raking soft light from the left. Palette: black, charcoal. No text, no labels.
```

Motion:

```text
The black wool lapel, the camera already sliding from the first frame. TIMELINE: 0 to 5 s, the camera slides slowly right along the lapel, the weave passing through focus, stopping on the button. CAMERA: macro slide of a few centimetres. CONTINUITY: the fabric and the button stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. AUDIO: a faint fabric rustle, no music. ENDING STATE: the button sharp at frame centre.
```

**Second family (Kling v3 Pro).** "From the opening frame" anchor, plain
verbs ("she walks two steps forward and turns to her left"), one keep line
for the garment, a "Sound:" line; two references at most (garment, face).

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The garment changes between frames | the user's on-model photo opens the look; slow motion; plain fabrics |
| The model's face changes between looks | the face reference on every clip; the face block verbatim in every still |
| Outfit transitions morph | hard cuts only |
| The mark and framing drift look to look | one framing line pasted in every look's still |
| Spins tear or swap garments | turns to profile, never past 180 degrees |
| Prints and logos crawl | avoid prints near the lens; no brand words |

## 10. Policy guards

- Garments shown are the garments sold: colour, length, fabric as in the
  user's photos; say which looks are approximations.
- The AI model is not a real model or celebrity; no likeness requests.
- No other houses' logos, monograms or signature prints in prompts.
- Size inclusivity is the user's casting choice; never alter a real
  model's body from a photo.
