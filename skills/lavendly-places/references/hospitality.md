# Recipe: hospitality (hotels, restaurants, cafés, bars, spas)

Hotel and resort films, room tours, restaurant and café reels, bar and
spa mood films, booking-page loops. The sale is the stay or the meal: how
it feels to arrive, sit, eat, sleep, look out. The venue's rooms, dishes
and views are shown from its own photos; AI adds atmosphere (steam, a
pour, candlelight, linen) and never a feature or a dish the venue does not
have.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: guests choosing where to stay or eat, on Instagram, TikTok,
  booking sites, Google profiles, the venue's own site.
- The viewer should picture the moment: the room at morning, the first
  bite, the view from the table, the quiet of the spa.
- The ending: the signature view or the table at golden hour (a photo),
  held 3 s, the call to action spoken ("Book your table for Friday."),
  space for the logo and booking card.

**60 s** (a stay, arrival to evening): arrival (the entrance, the lobby,
two photo scenes), the room (three scenes: room, view, bathroom), the day
(pool, a walk, lunch, three scenes), the spa (two), dinner (three), night
(the room lit, the view at blue hour). 14 to 16 scenes, venue features
from photos, atmosphere inserts between them.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the moment guests come for, in motion: the curtains lifting on the
  view, the flame on the grill, the pour, steam off the first plate.
- Payoff: the signature view or table at its best hour, still. The call to
  action names the booking.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 24mm glides and pedestals for rooms and terraces (photo scenes),
  85mm locked-off for atmosphere inserts, 50mm at 45 degrees for dishes.
  Atmosphere inserts are generic: hands, cups, glasses, linen, flame,
  water; never a recognisable room or dish the venue does not have.
- Light: hotels "soft morning light through sheer curtains", "golden hour
  on the terrace"; restaurants "warm pendant lights over each table,
  candles, the room in soft amber", "the open kitchen's fire as a warm
  practical"; spas "soft diffused light, steam, pale stone".
- Colour: warm and natural; true food colour; "Palette: amber, walnut,
  cream, deep green" (restaurant) or "white, sand, sea blue" (coastal
  hotel).

## 5. Sound

- Native: cutlery and murmur, a cork, a pour, sizzling, waves, a pool
  filter, birds at breakfast, a spa's water.
- Narration: 6 s up to 10 words or none; 15 s 25 to 32; 30 s 55 to 68; 60 s
  115 to 135. Moments, not amenities lists; facts as the venue gives them.
- Score: restaurant "Warm jazz trio feel around 92 BPM, brushed drums,
  upright bass, soft piano; low under the room, a small lift at the dish,
  ends on a piano phrase"; hotel "Light acoustic guitar and soft strings
  around 88 BPM, opening on the morning, warmer by evening". Instrumental;
  no recognisable standard.
- Mix: room and dining clips `mix` 0.4 (the murmur is the place); pour and
  sizzle inserts `native` 1 when no narration sits on them; score 0.3 with
  ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

Examples: the user's own place, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1: the room at morning** (photo scene; the still is for a concept)

Still:

```text
Interior photograph. Wide shot, 24mm lens, chest height, vertical lines straight. A hotel room with white walls, a low bed made with white linen, a rattan chair, tall glass doors open onto a balcony, sheer white curtains, the sea beyond. Soft morning light through the curtains. Palette: white, sand, sea blue. Nobody in frame. No text, no logo.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in toward the balcony doors over the whole shot, then the camera settles. The sheer curtains lift and fall once in a sea breeze; light ripples on the floor. The room, the bed, the chair and the doors stay exactly as in the opening frame; the sea stays the same view. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: soft waves, a distant gull. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: the wine pour** (atmosphere insert)

Still:

```text
Natural-light restaurant photograph. Close-up, 85mm lens, shallow focus. An empty clear wine glass on a dark walnut table beside a lit candle, the neck of a dark unlabelled bottle tilted above the glass at the top of frame, warm pendant light behind out of focus. Palette: amber, walnut, deep red. No text, no labels.
```

Motion:

```text
Locked-off camera. 0 to 3 s: red wine pours from the bottle into the glass in one smooth stream, swirling up the sides. 3 to 4 s: the bottle lifts out of frame and the wine settles. The glass, the candle and the table stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one glass, one bottle. SFX: a soft pour. Ambient noise: low restaurant murmur, cutlery. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the signature dish** (photo scene from the restaurant's own
photo; steam is the only addition)

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in toward the plate over the whole shot. Thin steam rises and curls slowly from the dish in the warm light. The plate, the food and its arrangement stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one plate. Ambient noise: soft restaurant murmur. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Seedance 1.5 Pro).** Production note, subject first,
"already in motion from the first frame" (the pour, the steam); for photo
scenes, CONTINUITY: "the room and its furniture stay exactly as in the
opening frame"; AUDIO with "no music".

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The dish changes ingredients or plating | the dish from the restaurant's photo; steam only |
| The room's view changes | "the sea stays the same view"; short push-in |
| Guests' faces melt in a wide | guests small and soft, or the room empty |
| Bottle labels or menus garbled | "unlabelled bottle", "a closed menu"; no words |
| Candle flames multiply | "one candle" with a count |
| Pours look like jelly | one smooth stream, under 4 s, bottle lifts out |

## 9. Policy guards

- The venue shown is the venue: rooms, views, pools, dishes and portions
  from its own photos; no feature, upgrade or dish it does not offer.
- AI people are never presented as the venue's staff, chefs or real
  guests; no reviews, ratings or awards unless the user gives them.
- Alcohol: adults over 25, no excess; follow the placement's rules.
- Prices, opening hours and offers only as the user gives them.
