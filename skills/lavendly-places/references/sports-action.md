# Recipe: sports and action

Training films, sport brand mood films, club and gym promos, outdoor
action (running, climbing, cycling, surfing), athlete-style portraits. The
sale is effort and the moment of release. Bodies in fast motion are where
video models fail most, so the craft is choosing moves models can hold:
one controlled movement, short clips, slow motion at the peak, the camera
still while the body moves.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: athletes and fans on TikTok, Reels, YouTube; clubs, gyms,
  races; sport brands.
- The viewer should feel the breath before, the burst, and the calm after.
- The ending: the athlete still after the effort (hands on knees, a look
  to the horizon), held 2 to 3 s, the call to action spoken ("Join the
  Saturday run."), space for the logo and date.

**60 s** (an athlete's story): struggle (rain training, three shots),
routine (four shots of preparation and drills), the moment (five shots
building to the peak in slow motion), the payoff (two shots of calm), the
line of truth (one held portrait). 15 to 18 shots.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the peak action in frame 1 (mid-stride, mid-air, the bag swinging
  from a punch) with its impact sound, then cut back to the build.
- Payoff: stillness after effort, held; the silence before the peak is the
  tension, the breath after it is the release.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 135mm telephoto at a low angle with the background compressed
  for speed; the camera locked-off while the athlete crosses the frame; a
  tracking alongside move only at a steady speed; 100mm macro for chalk,
  spikes, tape, sweat; handheld circling only for boxing and training.
  Speed reads from a still camera and a moving body, not from a moving
  camera.
- Slow motion: "slow motion for the first two seconds, then real time";
  keep slow motion under 3 s and on a simple movement.
- Light: "low evening sun backlighting the athlete, a rim along the
  shoulders, the stadium compressed and soft"; "hard top light in a dark
  gym, dust in the air"; "flat grey rain light".
- Colour: high contrast, deep blacks, desaturated with one accent (the
  kit's colour). "Palette: track red, black, warm white."

## 5. Sound

- Native: breath, spikes on the track, the starting gun, the bag's thud,
  the chain creak, wind, a crowd far off.
- Narration: short lines of 2 to 6 words. 6 s up to 8 words; 15 s 12 to
  20; 30 s 20 to 35; 60 s 60 to 100, with silence between lines.
- Score: "Hard-hitting electronic around 140 BPM, heavy kick and a rising
  synth. Builds through the preparation, cuts to silence for half a beat
  before the start, hits on the burst, drops to a low pad for the calm
  after. Instrumental."
- Mix: impact clips `native` 1 (the gun, the thud) with no narration on
  them; build clips `mix` 0.4; score 0.6 with ducking.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Kling)

Examples: the user's own place, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1: the start**

Still:

```text
Sports photograph. Low angle at track level, 135mm telephoto lens. A sprinter in a plain black singlet and black shorts crouched in starting blocks on a red track, fingers on the white line, backlit by low evening sun, sweat on her shoulders, the empty stadium compressed and soft behind. High contrast. Palette: track red, black, warm white. No text, no numbers, no logos.
```

Motion:

```text
From the opening frame, the sprinter drives out of the blocks and runs past the camera on the right. Slow motion for the first two seconds, then real time. The camera stays locked-off at track level. Her singlet stays plain black; the track, the light and the stadium stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one sprinter, nobody else on the track. Sound: a starting gun crack, spikes striking the track, a heavy breath. Clean frame, no text, no numbers.
```

**Pair 2: the heavy bag**

Still:

```text
Sports photograph. Medium close-up, 50mm lens. A boxer with short dark hair and taped hands in a grey t-shirt faces a black leather heavy bag in a dark gym, a single hard top light, dust in the air, sweat on his face. Desaturated, deep blacks. No text, no logos.
```

Motion:

```text
From the opening frame, the boxer throws three punches at the bag, left, right, left, at a steady rhythm; the bag swings back a little with each hit. The camera circles slightly to the left, handheld. His tape and t-shirt stay the same. Exactly one boxer, one bag. Sound: three thuds on the bag, breath, a chain creaking. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the ridge run**

Still:

```text
Sports photograph. Wide side view, 85mm lens. A trail runner in a faded green shirt and black shorts on a narrow ridge path at sunrise, mountains in layers of blue haze behind, low warm sun from the left. Palette: sage green, warm gold, blue haze. No text, no logos.
```

Motion:

```text
From the opening frame, the runner runs along the ridge path from left to right at a steady pace. The camera tracks alongside at the same speed, keeping him at the centre. His feet land on the path with each stride. Exactly one runner. Sound: footsteps on gravel, wind, steady breath. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Veo 3.1 Fast).** For macro and impact inserts: camera
first, timed beats, `SFX:` for the impact, "No music.", 4 s clips.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Limbs twist or swap mid-move | one movement per clip, locked-off camera, 4 to 5 s; slow motion on the peak |
| The ball teleports or doubles | avoid precise ball contact; show the wind-up and the result; "exactly one ball" |
| Jersey numbers and logos garble | "plain kit, no numbers, no logos" |
| The athlete changes between shots | the athlete block verbatim; a face reference within the cap |
| Fast camera plus fast body melts everything | a still camera and a moving body; a long lens sells speed |
| Crowds melt | the stadium soft and far, or empty |

## 9. Policy guards

- No real athletes, teams, leagues, crests, competitions or kit designs;
  no look-alikes of famous athletes.
- Safe practice: no dangerous stunts presented as easy, protective gear
  where the sport needs it, no doping imagery.
- No performance claims for a product or programme unless the user gives
  them with their basis.
- Youth sport: AI children only in non-identifying wides, never as the
  subject of claims.
