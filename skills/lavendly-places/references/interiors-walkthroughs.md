# Recipe: interiors and walkthroughs

Home tours, rental walkthroughs, office and studio spaces, show flats,
interior design portfolios, virtual staging. The sale is flow and light:
how one room opens into the next and how the day fills them. Every room is
its own photo and its own clip; the walls never move.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: buyers and renters, short-stay guests, office tenants,
  interior designers' clients; listing sites, Reels, YouTube.
- The viewer should be able to walk the place in their head: the order of
  rooms, where the light comes from, the best room.
- The ending: the best room or the view from it held 3 to 4 s, the call to
  action spoken, space for the address and agent card.

## 2. Structure by length

Gen is the length to generate on Veo (4 s); on Wan 2.7 generate 5. Every
scene is a photo scene; the room order follows the natural walk: entry,
living, kitchen, dining, bedrooms, bathroom, outdoor space.

**60 s**: every room in walking order, 4 s each (12 to 14 photo scenes), a
detail shot every third scene (materials, a window latch, light on a
floor), one time-passage shot (the living room from day to evening, a
separate evening photo), the best room last, held.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the best room first, moving toward its light or its view; the
  viewer's first thought is "I want to stand there".
- Payoff: the view or the outdoor space at the end, held; or the living
  room at evening with the lamps on.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: 16 to 24mm feel at chest height (about 1.2 to 1.5 m), verticals
  straight; gimbal glides forward or sideways at slow walking pace; slow
  pedestal over counters; slow pull-back reveals; 85mm slides for
  materials. One room per clip; the cut between rooms is a hard cut.
- Light: "bright soft overcast daylight from the windows, every lamp on,
  balanced"; bedrooms "soft morning light on the bed"; evenings "warm lamps,
  blue dusk in the windows" (only from evening photos).
- Colour: neutral white balance, bright, true colours, no heavy grade.
- Motion inside rooms: curtains moving slightly, a tree outside swaying,
  steam from a kettle, light shifting a little on the floor. Nothing that
  changes the room.

## 5. Sound

- Native: room tone, birds through an open window, a distant street, a
  clock, rain on glass.
- Narration: 6 s 10 to 13 words or none; 15 s 28 to 32; 30 s 55 to 68; 60
  s 115 to 135. Room by room, one feature and one feeling each, true facts
  spelled out.
- Score: "Soft piano and light strings around 90 BPM, airy and calm. Opens
  on a single piano phrase in the first room, a gentle pulse through the
  middle rooms, opens up on the terrace, ends on a held chord. Instrumental."
- Mix: every clip `mix` 0.3 under narration; score 0.3 with ducking; a
  music-only edit sets clips `off` and the score to 0.9.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

**Pair 1: the living room glide** (photo scene; the still is for a concept
or staged room)

Still:

```text
Interior photograph. Wide shot, 18mm lens, camera at chest height, vertical lines straight. The living room: a long open-plan room, pale oak floor, white walls, three floor-to-ceiling windows on the left wall onto a garden, a low linen sofa facing a stone fireplace on the right wall. Bright soft overcast daylight, every lamp on. Neutral white balance. Nobody in frame. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Smooth gimbal glide forward toward the windows at slow walking pace over the whole shot, then the camera settles. The curtains move slightly in a breeze; the garden trees sway outside. The walls, three windows, sofa and fireplace stay exactly as in the opening frame; vertical lines stay straight. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: a quiet room, distant birds outside. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: the kitchen counter**

Still:

```text
Interior photograph. Wide shot, 24mm lens, camera at chest height, vertical lines straight. A kitchen with a long honed white marble island with soft grey veins, two brushed brass taps over one sink, matte sage cabinets, one window on the far wall over the sink, three pendant lights over the island. Bright soft daylight, pendants on. Nothing on the island but a bowl of lemons. Nobody in frame. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
The camera trucks slowly to the right along the island at an even speed, then settles. Light slides across the marble as the angle changes. The island, the cabinets, the window and the three pendants stay exactly as in the opening frame; one bowl of lemons stays on the island. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: quiet kitchen, a kettle ticking as it cools. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the bedroom morning**

Still:

```text
Interior photograph. Wide shot, 18mm lens, camera at chest height. A calm bedroom with a low oak bed made with white linen, one tall window on the right wall with sheer curtains, a wool rug, a single ceramic lamp on an oak side table. Soft morning sun through the curtains across the bed. Neutral white balance. Nobody in frame. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in toward the bed over the whole shot. The sheer curtains lift slightly in a breeze; the patch of sun on the linen stays in place. The room, the bed, the window and the lamp stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody in frame. Ambient noise: soft birdsong outside, room tone. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Wan 2.7).** "Smooth glide forward, wide shot, wide lens,
eye level. Soft daylight, window light, low contrast, neutral colour
tone. The room keeps its exact layout, windows and furniture." For a
vertical Reel, ask for vertical photos and set `aspect` to 9:16.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| The room re-arranges or grows a new door | one room per 4 s clip; count the openings; trim before the change |
| The camera walks into a room the photo does not show | stay inside the photo's space; cut to the next room's photo |
| Furniture warps at the frame edges | 18 to 24mm, slower move, "no lens distortion" |
| A mirror shows a second camera or person | keep mirrors out of the move, or "the mirror reflects only the wall" |
| An unnamed shelf fills with objects | say what is on each surface, or "an empty oak shelf" |
| Lights switch off mid-shot | "every lamp stays on" |

## 9. Policy guards

- A real home shows only what is there: no larger rooms, no new windows,
  no better view, no removed defects. Virtual staging (furniture only) is
  labelled "virtually staged" by the user.
- Square metres, bedrooms and features only as the user gives them.
- Tenants' belongings and family photos stay out, or the user confirms
  consent.
- Offices: no implied occupancy or company names that are not real.
