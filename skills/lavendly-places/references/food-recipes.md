# Recipe: food and recipes

Recipe reels, cooking steps, a dish being made from start to plate, a
kitchen's craft. The sale is "I can make that, and I want to": a result
worth making and steps clear enough to follow. Hands only, one action per
clip, quantities countable and spoken, and the result never better than
the recipe can produce.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: home cooks on TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube; food brands'
  recipe content; a café's "how we make it".
- The viewer should crave the result in the first second and be able to
  follow every step.
- The ending: the finished dish at 45 degrees, a fork or spoon taking the
  first lift, held 2 to 3 s, the call to action spoken ("Save this for
  Sunday."), the full recipe in the post caption.

## 2. Structure by length

Gen is the length to generate on Veo (4 s); on Seedance generate 5. All
scenes are text-made unless the user supplies photos of their own dish.
Step shots share one top-down framing so the steps read as one board.

every step at 2 to 3 s with a macro insert every third step, a tip (one
held close-up, 4 s), plating, the first lift, a quiet bite cutaway (the fork
leaving frame, never a mouth chewing). 18 to 22 shots.

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the most indulgent moment first: the twist, the pull, the pour,
  the crunch, with its sound at 0 s.
- Payoff: the plated dish exactly as the steps produce it, the first lift,
  steam, held.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: top-down locked-off for every step, one framing for all of them
  (the same board, the same pan position, the same light, written once and
  pasted); 45 degrees at 85mm for the hero; 100mm macro for textures;
  50mm close-ups for the pan. Hands enter, do one thing, leave.
- Light: "a large soft window light from the top of frame, a soft
  backlight for steam, a white card fill"; moody: "one window light from
  the left, dark slate surface, black flags".
- Colour: warm, appetising, never blue; matte surfaces; "Palette: olive
  green, garlic white, chilli red, warm wood".
- Steam and liquids: "thin steam rising and curling slowly in the
  backlight", "oil shimmering, small bubbles around the garlic".

## 5. Sound

- Native: the sizzle, the chop, the boil, the toss, a spoon on ceramic.
  This is half the appeal: keep step clips `native` 1 when no narration sits
  on them, and leave gaps in the narration for the best sounds.
- Narration: imperative, quantities and verbs, numbers spelled out ("two
  cloves of garlic, sliced thin"). 6 s 6 to 10 words; 15 s 25 to 32; 30 s
  55 to 68; 60 s 115 to 135. Or none, with the recipe in the post caption.
- Score: "Light upbeat acoustic groove around 104 BPM, nylon guitar and
  shaker, low under the sizzles, a small lift on the plating, a short
  button at the end." Instrumental.
- Mix: step clips `mix` 0.6 under narration (the sizzle stays audible);
  hook and plating `native` 1; score 0.25 with ducking.
- Captions on (`tiktok` or `reels`): steps and quantities are what a muted
  viewer needs.

with fewer, fuller steps (8 shots) costs less; say so when the balance is
tight.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo)

**Pair 1: garlic into the oil**

Still:

```text
Natural-light food photograph. Top-down overhead shot, 35mm lens, camera pointing straight down. A black cast iron pan on a dark slate counter with a thin layer of olive oil shimmering in it, a small white bowl holding six thin slices of garlic at the top right of frame. Large soft window light from the top of frame. Palette: black, olive green, garlic white. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Static top-down camera. 0 to 1 s: a hand enters from the right and tips the bowl. 1 to 3 s: the six garlic slices slide into the oil and bubble gently around their edges. 3 to 4 s: the hand and bowl leave frame. The pan and the counter stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one pan, six garlic slices, one hand. SFX: a soft sizzle. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: the egg into the flour**

Still:

```text
Natural-light food photograph. Top-down overhead shot, 35mm lens. A wide white ceramic bowl on a light oak board holding a mound of flour with a small well in the centre, one brown egg resting beside the bowl, a wooden spoon at the bottom edge. Soft window light from the top of frame. Bright natural palette. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Static top-down camera. 0 to 2 s: a hand picks up the egg, cracks it on the bowl's rim and opens it over the well; the yolk drops into the flour. 2 to 4 s: the hand takes the spoon and stirs once in a slow circle. The bowl, the board and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one egg, one yolk, one hand. SFX: an egg crack, a spoon on ceramic. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the first lift**

Still:

```text
Natural-light food photograph. Close-up at 45 degrees, 85mm lens, shallow focus. A shallow white bowl of spaghetti glossy with olive oil, garlic slices, red chilli flakes and torn parsley, a fork resting in the pasta, steam rising, warm side light from the left with a soft backlight. Palette: warm wheat, olive, chilli red. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Slow push-in over the whole shot. 0 to 3 s: the fork twists slowly and lifts a nest of spaghetti out of the bowl, strands slipping off, steam curling in the backlight. 3 to 4 s: the fork leaves the top of frame. The bowl and its toppings stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one bowl, one fork. SFX: a soft twirl of pasta, a fork on ceramic. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Seedance 2.0 Fast).** Production note, "already in motion
from the first frame" (the slices already sliding), counts in CONTINUITY,
AUDIO "a soft sizzle, no music", ENDING STATE with the hand out of frame.

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Food morphs into another food | one action per clip; the ingredient named the same way in both prompts |
| Utensils merge with hands | one hand, one tool, one move; hands leave frame at the end |
| Knife cuts that do not cut | show the cut result, not the slicing |
| Quantities change between steps | counts in every prompt ("six garlic slices") |
| Steps framed differently | one top-down framing line pasted in every step still |
| Mouths chewing look wrong | never a bite close-up; the fork leaves frame |
| A blue cast | "warm side light, neutral white balance" |

## 9. Policy guards

- The dish shown is what the recipe makes: no extra garnish, sheen or
  portion the steps do not produce.
- Nutrition, "healthy", allergen-free or diet claims only as the user
  gives them.
- Raw meat and eggs handled safely on screen; no unsafe practices shown as
  normal.
- A café's or brand's own dish comes from its photos when sold as theirs.
