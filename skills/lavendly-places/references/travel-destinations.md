# Recipe: travel and destinations

Destination films, trip recaps from the user's photos, tourism board
mood films, travel creators' intros, "a day in" a city or coast. The sale
is longing: light, water, food, streets, the feeling of arriving. A real,
named place is shown from real photos where its landmarks appear; AI makes
mood shots of the kind of place, never claims to be the famous view.

## 1. Purpose and ending

- Audience: travellers dreaming and planning on Reels, TikTok, YouTube;
  tourism boards; tour and stay operators.
- The viewer should want to be there at a specific hour: the light, the
  sound, the taste.
- The ending: a quiet wide at sunset or blue hour held 3 s, the call to
  action spoken ("Plan your week by the sea."), space for the place name
  card the user adds.

## 2. Real place or mood

| Shot | Real destination sold as itself | Mood film of a kind of place |
|---|---|---|
| Landmarks, skylines, famous views | the user's photo as first frame, or left out | never named; generic ("a whitewashed fishing village") |
| Streets, food, textures, water, sky | AI mood shots are fine, generic and true to the kind of place | AI |
| People | distant, from behind, or one named traveller | the same |
| Label | none needed for real photos; AI mood shots inside a real destination film are fine when they invent no feature | "mood film" when it could be read as a specific place |

**60 s** (a personal journey): anticipation (packing, a train window, two
shots), arrival (aerial, lane, two shots), four experiences of two shots
each (swim, market, food, boat), a quiet reflection (a held wide, 5 s),
sunset, blue hour, the call to action. 14 to 16 shots; real landmarks from
the user's photos only.

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the most iconic or unexpected view in motion (a slow advance over
  the harbour, a door opening onto the sea, a first-person step into a
  lane), or a sensory close-up with its sound (a sizzle, waves).
- Payoff: a still, wide moment at sunset or blue hour with a small figure
  from behind; the viewer steps into the figure's place.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Camera: slow drone advances and gentle 45-degree orbits for scale, POV
  walks at walking pace for presence, 85mm close-ups for food and hands,
  locked-off wides for reflection. Alternate families so no two
  neighbours share a move.
- A montage option on Veo: one 8 s clip with four 2 s bracketed shots
  (`[00:00-00:02] ...`) gives four views for one clip's price; only the
  first matches the opening image, so use it for textures and mood, never
  for a real landmark.
- Light: "late afternoon, warm low sun from the right", "bright midday sun,
  turquoise water, short shadows", "blue hour, warm lanterns lit", "soft
  morning light".
- Colour: warm and saturated but natural skin and water; "avoid an orange
  sky"; "Palette: white, turquoise, terracotta".

## 6. Sound

- Native: waves, gulls, a boat motor, market chatter, sizzling, church
  bells far off, cicadas.
- Narration: optional; first person or none. 6 s none; 15 s 25 to 32; 30 s
  55 to 68; 60 s 115 to 135. Senses, not adjectives ("Salt on the bread.
  Bells at six.").
- Score: "Warm acoustic guitar and light hand percussion around 112 BPM,
  bright and moving. Opens with the aerial, keeps a walking rhythm through
  the day, slows and thins at sunset, ends on a single guitar chord at blue
  hour. Instrumental; no recognisable folk tune."
- Mix: ambience-led clips `mix` 0.5 (waves under the score); food clips
  `native` 1 for the sizzle when no narration sits on them; score 0.4 with
  ducking.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Veo)

Examples: the user's own place, brand and photos replace the ones shown.

**Pair 1: the harbour from the air**

Still:

```text
Travel photograph. High aerial view, 24mm lens, looking down at about thirty degrees. A small whitewashed fishing village on a rocky coast in late afternoon, blue wooden boats in a stone harbour, turquoise water, terracotta roofs, warm low sun from the right. Palette: white, turquoise, terracotta. No text, no signs.
```

Motion:

```text
High aerial drone shot slowly advancing over the harbour toward the village, height steady, then the camera settles. The boats rock gently on small ripples; a gull crosses the lower frame once. The village, the harbour wall and the coastline stay exactly as in the opening frame. Nobody large in frame. Ambient noise: waves, gulls, a distant boat motor. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 2: the night market wok**

Still:

```text
Travel photograph. Medium shot, 35mm lens, eye level. A street food stall at a night market, a cook in a white t-shirt tossing noodles in a large wok over a high flame, red paper lanterns overhead, steam rising, warm tungsten light. Palette: red, amber, black. No text, no readable signs.
```

Motion:

```text
Handheld camera moving slowly closer to the wok. 0 to 2 s: the cook tosses the noodles once and the flame flares up. 2 to 4 s: he tosses them again and steam rolls up through the lantern light. The stall, the cook and the lanterns stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one cook. SFX: wok sizzle, metal scraping. Ambient noise: market chatter. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Pair 3: the traveller at blue hour**

Still:

```text
Travel photograph. Wide shot, 35mm lens, eye level. A woman in a linen shirt sits from behind on a low stone harbour wall at blue hour, the sea calm, the village lights warm across the water, a deep blue sky. She is small in the lower left third. Palette: deep blue, warm gold, stone grey. No text.
```

Motion:

```text
Static locked-off camera. She turns her head slowly to the right toward the lights and stays still. Small waves lap the wall; one light comes on across the water. The wall, the sea and the village stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one woman, nobody else near. Ambient noise: lapping water, distant voices, a bell far off. No music. Clean frame, no text.
```

**Second family (Wan 3.0) for long takes.** "Subject plus scene plus
motion" for a 15 s continuous aerial: "The camera advances slowly over the
harbour for the whole take, height steady; boats rock; gulls cross."
Then the aesthetic words (sunset, warm colour tone, wide lens, aerial).
Wan 3.0 sends two references at most.

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| A famous landmark is drawn wrong | the user's photo as the first frame, or leave it out and keep the shot generic |
| Crowds with melted faces | people distant, soft and few; or nobody |
| The sky turns orange | "avoid an orange sky", "a clear pale blue sky" |
| The village changes during the aerial | slow advance, 4 s, no orbit past 45 degrees |
| Water freezes or loops | name its motion: "small ripples", "waves lapping the wall" |
| Readable signs in another script | "no readable signs" |
| Timestamp montage drifts from the opening | use it only for mood textures, never for the hero view |

## 10. Policy guards

- Do not invent features of a real place (a beach, a view, a landmark,
  a season) or show AI shots as a specific real hotel, beach or attraction.
- Avoid naming real landmarks in prompts; real landmarks come from photos.
- Local people are not props: no identifiable locals without consent; no
  stereotyped costumes or customs.
- Prices, travel times and "best" claims only as the user gives them.
