# Recipe: faceless explainer

The narrated knowledge short with no presenter: a voice, a picture per
sentence, one look, one score. YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels and the
"faceless channel" on long-form YouTube. Worked example throughout: why
octopuses have three hearts. The user's own topic replaces it.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: one surprising fact made clear enough to repeat, in a format a
  channel can make every day with the same look and voice.
- Audience: curious viewers scrolling with sound on (Shorts, TikTok) or
  muted (Reels, feeds); they decide in about two seconds.
- The ending delivers the answer the hook promised, shown on screen, then
  one loop line that feeds back into the first sentence or a single open
  question for the next part. No "follow for more" outro.

## 2. Structures

Columns: shot size and angle, lens feel, the one move, what the picture
shows, the model length (Veo 3.1 Fast) and the trim, the word budget of the
line under it.

### 90 s (chapters)

The 60 s plan plus a 30 s middle chapter between shots 7 and 8: "blue
blood" (macro of blue-tinted water around a shell by analogy, said as an
analogy, 3 shots of 4 s), "the cost" (a slow tired drift down to the sand,
8 s hold), "the trade" (crawling past a fish that darts, 2 shots of 4 s).
About 200 words. Over 90 s: split into two parts, part 1 ending on "So why
would a body stop its own heart?"

## 3. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the strangest true sentence, five words or fewer, over the most
  legible frame of the subject (an eye, a hand, the object itself). Spoken
  from 0.0 s; captions show it at once.
- Curiosity chain: every sentence opens a small question the next sentence
  closes. "Three hearts." Why three? "Two for the gills." And the third?
- Payoff at 80 to 90 percent: the answer shown, static shot.
- Loop line: the last sentence leads into the first ("... which is why it
  has three hearts") so a replay feels like a continuation.

## 4. Camera, light, colour

- One look for the whole channel: the channel kit is the style line, the
  light recipe, the voice, the score palette and the caption style, written
  once and reused on every episode.
- Rhythm: change shot size every cut; adjacent shots never share a move;
  locked-off is the default, a slow push-in for the hook, static for the
  payoff.
- Light, nature: "sunlight from above through the water, soft rippling
  caustics". Light, studio: "one soft key from the left, white sweep
  background, gentle falloff".
- Colour: "muted blue-green water, warm red-brown subject"; three to five
  palette anchors repeated in every still prompt.
- Vertical framing: subject in the band from 15 to 55 percent of frame
  height; nothing important in the bottom third (captions, platform UI).

## 5. Sound

- Narration: 2.4 words per second; brian, george or alice; one voice for
  the channel.
- Native: every clip `mix` 0.2 (underwater hush, room tone); "No music." in
  every motion prompt.
- Score brief: "Curious and light: soft marimba and a low bowed bass at
  about 80 BPM. Opens on one plucked note under the hook, builds a gentle
  pulse through the facts, settles on a held chord under the last line.
  Instrumental, sparse." Volume 0.3, ducking on.
- Captions: `tiktok` on 9:16 (three words per chunk, bold), `youtube` on
  16:9.

## 7. Prompt pairs (Veo 3.1 Fast)

**Pair 1, scene 1, the hook.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Nature documentary, observational, natural light. A common octopus with mottled red-brown skin and one gold eye with a horizontal slit pupil rests on a rock ledge in a clear shallow reef, arms tucked under its body. Extreme close-up on the eye, 100mm macro look, focus plane millimetres deep. Sunlight from above through the water, soft rippling caustics on the rock. Blue-green water behind, out of focus. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Nature documentary, observational, natural light, one continuous take. Extreme close-up, macro. 0 to 2 s: the eye is still while caustic light ripples over the skin. 2 to 4 s: the skin around the eye darkens from red-brown to deep red and the body swells once, slowly. Camera: locked-off, no movement. End state: the eye looks straight ahead, the skin deep red. The octopus, the rock and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one octopus in frame. Ambient noise: soft underwater hush. No music. Clean frame: no text, no subtitles, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Pair 2, scene 3, the jet.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Nature documentary, observational, natural light. A common octopus with mottled red-brown skin crouches on the edge of a coral rock above open sand in clear shallow water, arms gathered behind its head. Medium shot from the side at the octopus's height, 35mm look, moderate depth of field. Sunlight from above, soft caustics on the sand. Open blue water fills the right half of the frame. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Nature documentary, observational, natural light, one continuous take. Medium shot, side view. 0 to 1 s: the body fills with water. 1 to 3 s: the octopus jets off the rock to the right, arms trailing straight behind it, a small puff of sand lifting from the ledge. 3 to 4 s: it leaves the frame on the right and the sand settles. Camera: locked-off, no movement. End state: the empty ledge and the sand settling. The rock, the sand and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one octopus, no other animal. SFX: a short rush of water. Ambient noise: underwater hush. No music. Clean frame: no text, no subtitles, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Second family, Wan 2.7 (image to video: motion plus camera only).**

```text
The skin around the eye darkens from red-brown to deep red, the body swells once and settles. Static shot, locked-off camera. Extreme close-up, soft top light through water, low contrast, cool colour tone. Exactly one octopus. Sound: soft underwater hush. No music.
```

## 8. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wrong arm count, tentacles melting | Frame so the arms are tucked or partly out of frame; slow motion only; "arms trailing straight behind" |
| Caustic light flickers or boils | "soft rippling caustics", one light source, locked-off camera |
| The look drifts between episodes | The channel kit pasted verbatim; a style reference on Wan 2.7 or Seedance |
| Every shot is a pretty reef with no claim | Rewrite each beat from its sentence (words-off test) |
| A presenter's face or hands appear uninvited | "Exactly one octopus, no other animal, no person" |
| Text or a channel name painted in frame | Clean-frame line; titles are added in the app |
| Hook frame is a wide reef | Open on the eye; the wide comes second |
| Fact drift across 90 s | Keep to settled facts; the user confirms any number |

## 9. Policy guards

- Never clone a creator's channel: the format (narrated facts, one look) is
  free to use; their voice, name, look, script and footage are not.
- Facts: settled common knowledge stated plainly, or the user's source.
  Mark analogies as analogies ("think of it as ...").
- Animals behave as the species does; no invented behaviour.
- Stylised footage of real phenomena is fine; never present it as recorded
  footage of a specific real event.
