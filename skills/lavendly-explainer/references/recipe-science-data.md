# Recipe: science and data

A mechanism, a finding or a set of numbers made understandable and honest.
Science channels, research communication, reports turned into video.
Worked example: how little of Earth's water we can drink. The user's own
subject replaces it.

## 1. Purpose, audience, ending

- Purpose: the viewer leaves with the right size of a number and the reason
  behind it, not a list of figures.
- Audience: curious general viewers, students, stakeholders of a report.
- The ending gives the one number that matters as a spoken sentence over
  its physical comparison, and what it means for the viewer.

## 2. Honesty with numbers (binding)

- The model never draws a chart, a graph, an axis, a percentage, an
  equation or a labelled diagram. Every number is spoken and appears in the
  captions from the narration.
- Physical comparisons show direction and rough scale (a full tank against
  one glass), never a precise value. Say "about" in the narration when the
  picture approximates.
- Every specific number comes from the user's source, or is a settled,
  widely published figure. Name the source in the narration when it is a
  study or a report ("according to the agency's 2025 report"). No source,
  no number: ask.
- Illustrative figures are called illustrative aloud ("example figures").
- Ranges and uncertainty are said ("between two and three degrees").
- Correlation is not causation: "people who do X also tend to Y", never
  "X causes Y" unless the source says so.
- A real chart the user has is built outside Lavendly, exported as a video
  and imported (`import_asset`), then placed as a ready clip; or the user
  adds it in the app. Never ask a model to animate a chart.

Add a "how we know" chapter (20 s: a sample bottle filled at a lake shore,

## 4. Hook and payoff

- Hook: the surprising scale, as the smallest or largest physical thing in
  the film ("All the water you drink is this.").
- Payoff: the same object at the end with the number said once more and
  what it means; static.

## 5. Camera, light, colour

- Style line: "Macro science documentary, clean studio light". Backgrounds
  plain: "a seamless white sweep", "a dark grey background".
- Light: "one soft key from the left, a thin backlight so the water glows";
  water and glass need a backlight to read.
- Moves: locked-off for every comparison (the viewer must see the change);
  slow pull-back to reveal scale; slow truck to compare side by side.
- Colour: "neutral white balance, clear water, cool blue-white ice"; colour
  carries meaning (salt water slightly cloudy, fresh water clear).

## 6. Sound

- Narration 2.4 words per second, numbers spelled out; brian, alice or
  matilda.
- Native `mix` 0.3: pours, drips, crackles are part of the explanation.
- Score: "Clean and precise: soft synth pulses and a felt piano at about 85
  BPM, opening with a single drop-like note, adding layers as the numbers
  shrink, ending on one quiet tone under the last drop." Volume 0.25,
  ducking on.
- Captions: `youtube` on 16:9, `reels` on 9:16; the numbers read in the
  captions as the voice says them.

## 8. Prompt pairs (Veo 3.1 Fast)

**Pair 1, the comparison.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Macro science documentary, clean studio light. A large clear glass tank full of still water stands on a seamless white sweep, an empty small drinking glass to its right, both plain and unlabelled. Medium shot from the side at table height, 50mm look, deep focus. One soft key from the left and a thin backlight so the water glows faintly. Neutral white balance. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Macro science documentary, clean studio light, one continuous take. Medium shot. 0 to 1 s: everything is still. 1 to 3 s: a thin stream of water rises from the tank's tap at the base into the small glass, filling it to the brim. 3 to 4 s: the stream stops and the surface of the small glass settles flat. Camera: locked-off, no movement. End state: the large tank still nearly full, the small glass full beside it. The tank, the glass, the sweep and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one tank and one glass, no hands, no person. SFX: a thin trickle of water. No music. Clean frame: no text, no subtitles, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Pair 2, the drop.**

Still-frame prompt:

```text
Macro science documentary, clean studio light. The tip of a clear glass pipette holds one hanging drop of water above a shallow glass dish of still water on a dark grey background. Macro shot from the side, 100mm macro look, focus plane millimetres deep on the drop. One soft key from the left, a thin backlight that makes the drop glow. Clean frame, no text.
```

Motion prompt:

```text
Macro science documentary, clean studio light, one continuous take. Macro, side view. 0 to 1 s: the drop swells at the tip. 1 to 2 s: it falls straight down into the dish. 2 to 4 s: a single ring of ripples spreads to the edge of the dish and fades. Camera: locked-off, no movement. End state: the dish surface calm again, the pipette tip empty. The pipette, the dish and the light stay exactly as in the opening frame. Exactly one drop. SFX: one small clear drip. No music. Clean frame: no text, no subtitles, no on-screen graphics.
```

**Second family, Seedance 1.5 Pro (production note).**

```text
STARTING STATE: a pipette tip with one hanging drop above a shallow dish of water, dark grey background, backlight. TIMELINE: [0-1s] the drop swells. [1-2s] it falls straight down into the dish. [2-5s] one ring of ripples spreads to the edge and fades. CAMERA: macro side view, locked-off. AUDIO: one small clear drip, no music. ENDING STATE: calm surface, empty tip, held.
```

## 9. Failure modes and fixes

| Failure | Fix |
|---|---|
| A bar chart or a percentage appears | Remove "data", "chart", "percent" from prompts; the comparison objects only |
| The glass fills to the wrong level | Do not depend on a level; the narration carries the value |
| Liquid behaves like jelly | Short action, "a thin stream", "settles flat", backlight; one liquid event per clip |
| Scale objects change size between shots | The same objects named verbatim; a reference image on Seedance or Wan |
| Labels on bottles and glassware | "plain and unlabelled" |
| A number in the script the user did not supply | Remove it or ask for the source |

## 10. Policy guards

- No invented numbers, studies or quotes. Sources named when specific.
- No AI picture used as evidence of a result (a cell healing, a body
  changing, a crop growing faster).
- Health and medicine: general information only, "talk to a doctor" when
  the user's audience could act on it; never a dosage.
- Physical comparisons are said to be comparisons.
